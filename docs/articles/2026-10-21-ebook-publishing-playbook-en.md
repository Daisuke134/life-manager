# How eBooks Become a Publishing Business

*Internal research draft. Researched 21 October 2026. This article summarizes public mechanisms in original language; it does not reproduce creator scripts, videos, likenesses, covers, or other creative assets. Revenue and audience claims are labeled by their evidence source.*

## The useful lesson is the business model, not a copied script

Four publishing models show how an eBook can be more than a file on a storefront:

- **Tommi Pedruzzi** describes demand-first Amazon publishing, a connected book catalog, and educational posts that route readers to training.
- **Yang Mun** combines recurring short-form spiritual content with focused direct-sale pages, instant delivery, bundles, and a guided program.
- **Joanna Penn** documents a long-running, multi-title business across ebook, print, audio, retailers, and direct sales.
- **Pierre Jeanty** is presented as a direct-to-reader operator using a Shopify store and targeted marketing.
- **Katie Cross** describes a direct store that combines digital fulfillment, email automation, and print-on-demand.
- **Dave Chesson** reports survey responses from 547 direct-selling authors, giving a broader but self-selected view of traffic and product patterns.
- **David Gaughran** publishes a practical end-to-end guide that emphasizes reader demand, niche, editing, metadata, distribution, and marketing.

Their mechanisms can inform a new publishing system. Their exact writing, videos, visuals, testimonials, and persona assets are not a reusable content library. The business system below uses original Anicca materials and verified customer evidence.

## How strong is the evidence?

Tommi’s X posts are first-party statements, not audited royalty or bank records. Yang Mun’s product pages are first-party descriptions and checkout offers, not sales statements. Their public follower, reader, and testimonial counters do not establish paid orders or profit. Joanna Penn’s reports are unusually detailed first-party disclosures with periods, units, and format/vendor breakdowns, but they are still self-reported. The Pierre Jeanty material is an interview, not an audited financial statement.

The supplied screenshots show substantial public reach for the two accounts: one Tommi post displayed about 1.31 million views at the screenshot time, and the Yang Mun Instagram profile showed about 2.52 million followers and 432 posts. Those figures are point-in-time audience signals. They do not tell us how many viewers clicked, bought, kept a subscription, or generated settled profit.

## Case 1: Tommi Pedruzzi — demand, catalog, and a teaching funnel

In posts dated 20 October 2026, Pedruzzi described building a three-book portfolio over 90 days. The sequence he presents is: choose a persistent problem, narrow it to a specific reader, check marketplace demand and review gaps, use AI to accelerate drafting and production, publish through KDP, then track sales and refine. One of the posts routes readers who comment with a keyword to a course or masterclass. A separate post frames publishing as a brand that grows with each new release. [90-day portfolio post](https://x.com/TommiPedruzzi/status/2112472893296718309), [book-brand post](https://x.com/TommiPedruzzi/status/2112562562537800055)

His more detailed thread recommends checking a decade-long trend line, searching Amazon categories and ranks, and reading three-star reviews to identify missing explanations. It then describes AI-assisted outlining, drafting, editing, cover creation, and KDP publication. This is a useful **sequence to investigate**, not proof that his rank cutoffs, price recommendations, timeline, or output quality transfer to every category. Amazon sales rank is a moving category signal, not a universal conversion forecast. [Tommi’s October 20 thread](https://x.com/TommiPedruzzi/status/2112581258740170760)

The same thread reports $577,000 from one book in 12 months, while other first-party posts on the profile make separate seven-figure and $700,000-in-11-month claims. No royalty statement, title-level sales report, bank record, or independent audit was available in the reviewed material. Keep each figure labeled **self-reported and unverified**; do not use it as an earnings forecast. [The $577,000 post and thread](https://x.com/TommiPedruzzi/status/2112562562537800055)

Pedruzzi also lists possible formats and channels: ebook, paperback, hardcover, audio, Kindle Unlimited page reads, translation, bundles, and other retailers. These are potential product routes, not nine automatic revenue streams. Each has its own rights, production, eligibility, delivery, and accounting work. His post also suggests an eventual KDP-account sale; that is not part of this playbook without a current official terms review. [Nine-stream post](https://x.com/TommiPedruzzi/status/2105893038648430838)

**Mechanisms worth adapting:** research a durable buyer problem before drafting; use negative reviews as a research signal without copying their wording; build a coherent catalog rather than relying on one title; make educational posts lead to one relevant next step; measure actual orders and royalties.

**Do not copy:** the exact thread, sales arithmetic, rank thresholds as universal rules, unsupported “anyone can earn” claims, or the recommendation to procure 15–25 reviews through groups. Amazon allows free or discounted reader copies only when a review is not required and the review is not influenced. [KDP customer-review policy](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G202101910)

## Case 2: Yang Mun — repeated short-form identity and a direct offer ladder

The supplied Instagram screenshot shows a consistent monk-centered feed, short videos with on-screen captions, and profile highlights for books, questions, healing practices, and daily routines. The website links to Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook. Firecrawl could not retrieve TikTok or Instagram feeds, so no complete posting cadence, top-post ranking, watch-time pattern, or cross-post history was independently established. Treat the screenshot as a limited visual observation, not a full channel audit. [Yang Mun’s site](https://yang-mun.com/), [Instagram profile](https://www.instagram.com/yangmunus), [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@Yangmunv2)

The store is more informative about the offer system. A reader can buy one focused PDF, such as *Time To Heal*, for $12.99 at the observed sale price; its page offers instant email delivery and Inner Circle access. Other pages offer individual ebooks, a 30-day guided program at $49.99, and a program-plus-books collection at $59.99. A separate bundle page presents a larger group of ebooks, audiobooks, a journal, a breathwork guide, and community access at $25. These pages describe different bundles and prices, so the numbers should not be collapsed into one price ladder without matching the exact SKU and contents. [Time To Heal](https://yang-mun.com/products/time-to-heal-the-wisdom-of-yang-mun), [30-day journey](https://yang-mun.com/products/30-day-healing-journey-yang-mun), [journey plus ebooks](https://yang-mun.com/products/the-complete-collection-yang-mun), [bundle page](https://yang-mun.com/pages/bundle)

The visible funnel is: short-form or owned content → profile link → problem-specific sales page → direct Shopify checkout → instant digital delivery → community/onboarding → optional larger collection or guided program. The pages use a clear problem, contents, reviews/testimonials, a primary purchase action, and answers to delivery questions. The site also displays follower/reader/journey counters and testimonials; those are seller-published signals, not independently verified sales or typical outcomes. [Journey page](https://yang-mun.com/pages/journey)

**Mechanisms worth adapting:** make the digital product easy to understand and receive; give the first book a single clear reader problem; offer a genuinely additive bundle or guided practice after the entry product; use repeatable series and a consistent visual identity; make the next step easy to find.

**Do not copy:** the monk’s face, voice, script, captions, page design, testimonials, or medical efficacy claims. Yang Mun’s pages discuss symptoms such as migraines, digestion, pain, and sleep. Anicca’s own neuroscience or wellness statements need evidence from the approved-claims source before they appear in automated scripts or product pages.

## Case 3: Joanna Penn — backlist, formats, and transparent reporting

Penn’s 2016 report gives a specific 12-month period: May 2015 through April 2016. She reported 40,667 paid copies and $95,000 in book-sales income, with Amazon accounting for 56% and Apple plus Kobo for 40% in that report. She described using KDP, Kobo Writing Life, Apple Books, ACX for audio, and Draft2Digital for other ebook distribution. The figures are first-party, period-defined reporting; they are not an external audit. [2016 book-sales breakdown](https://www.thecreativepenn.com/2016/06/03/breakdown-book-sales/)

Her 2021 report covered May 2020 through April 2021. Penn said book sales were a little over six figures in GBP and represented 34% of company income, with income also coming from courses, affiliate activity, and podcast sponsorship. She reported books selling in 126 countries and cautioned that results differ by genre, catalog size, business model, and marketing spend. [2021 book-sales and revenue breakdown](https://www.thecreativepenn.com/2021/07/03/book-sales-revenue-breakdown-2021/)

In 2022, she documented a Shopify direct store alongside marketplace distribution. The useful pattern is **wide availability plus an owned reader relationship**, with direct product pages and multiple formats. The operational lesson is also to report sales by period, country, vendor, and format instead of describing total revenue as one undifferentiated number. [Minimum viable direct store](https://www.thecreativepenn.com/2022/08/12/selling-books-direct-with-shopify-minimum-viable-store/)

## Case 4: Pierre Jeanty — direct reader relationships

The Creative Penn’s 2023 interview presents Jeanty as a seven-figure direct-sales book operator. The headline is a claim in the interview; the page does not provide audited net revenue. The transferable advice is to own the reader relationship and customer data, make the store and landing pages work before spending on ads, and separate distinct reader brands where that improves targeting. Because this account discusses advertising, it is a model for offer and customer ownership, not a reason to buy ads before Anicca has a cost-complete sales baseline. [Interview with Pierre Jeanty](https://www.thecreativepenn.com/2023/02/27/how-to-build-a-seven-figure-book-business-selling-direct-to-readers-with-pierre-jeanty/)

## Three more useful operating references

**Katie Cross** discusses selling ebooks, audiobooks, and print books directly through Shopify, using BookFunnel for digital delivery and print-on-demand partners for physical books. The interview also covers email automation, customer data, shipping, and taxes. It describes the workflow and tradeoffs; it is not an audited revenue case. [Katie Cross on Shopify direct sales](https://www.thecreativepenn.com/2022/06/20/selling-books-direct-on-shopify/)

**Dave Chesson / Kindlepreneur** published a direct-sales study updated 13 October 2026. The page reports 547 survey responses; it says respondents cited social media (42%), email marketing (23%), paid ads (16%), and content marketing (10%) as website traffic sources. The author explicitly warns that survey respondents may not represent all authors. Field dates, sampling method, and some denominators were not stated in the retrieved page. These results can inform channel coverage, but they are not a causal ranking or an Anicca forecast. [Direct-sales survey](https://kindlepreneur.com/the-truth-on-selling-books-direct-insights-from-authors/)

**David Gaughran**’s 2026 guide lays out an end-to-end independent publishing process: write for a known readership, define niche and branding, edit and format, price for the genre, optimize metadata, choose direct and distributor routes, and plan marketing and reader reviews. This is a useful workflow reference, not a financial results report. Its simplified royalty statements should be checked against current official KDP rules before pricing. [Self-publishing guide](https://davidgaughran.com/lets-get-digital-how-to-self-publish-guide-free/)

## A combined publishing system for Anicca

### Product

The current English offer is *The Anicca Reset*: 49 short chapters delivered as an instant-access PDF, currently shown at $10.99. The page also offers a free three-day email series and a Daily Anicca Letter with a 14-day free trial followed by $9.99 per month. This already provides an entry book and a recurring follow-on product. [Current product page](https://aniccaai.com/monk)

Use the current book as the first catalog asset. In parallel, build a slate of distinct, original English book concepts around specific reader problems, using durable search interest and marketplace reviews as research inputs. A new title must answer a different problem with its own complete value; splitting the same thin material into several listings is not a catalog strategy. Map claims to evidence and make the reader benefit concrete before generating a manuscript.

Use direct sales for instant PDF delivery, the email relationship, and bundles whose added material is already complete. Use Amazon KDP for marketplace discovery and eligible print/ebook formats after checking rights, pricing, production cost, and royalties. KDP Select is a 90-day program for Kindle ebooks with digital exclusivity requirements; do not enroll the same ebook while selling that digital book directly. [KDP Select](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G200798990), [KDP Select requirements](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/GD9PMU58BV24QFZ7)

KDP royalties are conditional. The 70% option has eligibility and list-price requirements, while the 35% option applies in other situations; delivery cost and territory can affect the calculation. Recalculate from the current official rules for each edition and territory rather than repeating a creator’s simplified “70% on most price points” line. [KDP ebook royalties](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G200644210)

### Distribution

Treat every channel as a route to the same owned offer, with a format native to that channel:

| Channel | Original format | Reader next step | Primary evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | Short vertical lesson with a specific problem and one practice | Tracked Anicca Reset link | Postiz receipt, watched-video metrics where available, tracked clicks, paid orders |
| Instagram Reels | Platform-edited short video and saved series/highlights | Tracked product or free-letter link | Connected account, post receipt, reach, clicks, purchases |
| YouTube Shorts and long-form | Short lesson, then deeper explanation or book walkthrough | Description/pinned link to the owned page | Video analytics and tracked orders |
| X | Educational thread, niche research note, launch update | Keyword reply or tracked direct link | Post receipt, link clicks, checkout and orders |
| Blog and search | Original problem-solving articles with source notes | Relevant book sample or free three-day letter | Search visits, tracked clicks, orders |
| Email | Free three-day introduction, delivery, and useful daily letters | Paid letter or related book | Delivery, paid subscription, cancellation, refunds, settled receipts |
| Amazon and other retailers | Kindle, print-on-demand, later audio/translation where justified | Marketplace purchase | Official unit, royalty, KENP, refund, and payout reports |

The existing English TikTok schedule is 08:00 and 21:00 JST. Use that schedule for immediate distribution once its current occurrence fence, render readiness, and TikTok disclosure payload are resolved; there is no separate low-volume pilot gate. Add Instagram when the account is connected inside the current plan. Add other channels through their own correct owner, account, and tracked-link route; do not borrow unrelated Anicca or Honne accounts just to increase the channel count.

### Content format

Build an original content library around repeated reader questions. A short video can use this sequence: a precise situation the reader recognizes → one useful insight → one small practice → a low-pressure next step. An X thread can expand a single question into evidence, steps, caveats, and a link. A blog article can preserve the research, explain what is known and unknown, and link to the relevant product. Reuse the **idea and evidence**, then write and edit each platform version for its own audience.

No reliable posting frequency for Tommi or Yang Mun was established from the available public feed evidence. Keep Anicca’s existing schedule while reviewing channel receipts; do not claim that the competitors require a particular daily count.

### Measurement

Keep the existing attribution chain and add no parallel analytics database: creative ID and platform → owned redirect → landing view → checkout start → paid purchase → refund/fee/settlement → attributable production and platform cost → contribution margin. Record views and likes as discovery measures, not sales. Amazon rank is a demand clue, not an order receipt. Calculate each title and channel over a consistent time period and currency.

## Platform and content rules that change the playbook

- TikTok requires the content-disclosure setting when a post promotes your own business; it labels that post as promotional content. Realistic AI-generated video, audio, or imagery also needs the AI-generated label. The English eBook’s current source sends both TikTok commercial toggles as false while its AI flag is true, so the actual payload must be corrected before promotional publishing. [TikTok business-promotion disclosure](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/promoting-a-brand-product-or-service), [TikTok AI-generated labels](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content)
- KDP requires disclosure of AI-generated text, images, and translations; AI-assisted editing/brainstorming is treated differently. The publisher still remains responsible for factual accuracy and intellectual-property rights. [KDP AI content guidance](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G200672390)
- Never condition a free or discounted book copy on a review or try to influence what the reader writes. [KDP review policy](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G202101910)
- Do not reuse another creator’s scripts, captions, video, voice, face, covers, testimonials, or unique page design. Extract a business mechanism, then make the creative from Anicca’s own product facts, approved claims, and original assets.
- Do not present symptom relief, neuroscience, audience size, earnings, or reader outcomes as established unless Anicca has a source that substantiates that exact claim.

## Open-source publishing standard

The reusable asset is the research method: source register, evidence grade, product/claim map, channel templates, attribution schema, and cost/revenue readback. Publish those original materials under the repository’s MIT license. Keep creators’ copyrighted expression and private/customer information out of the package. The research article itself is an internal draft; this commit does not publish a public marketing page or a post to any social account.
