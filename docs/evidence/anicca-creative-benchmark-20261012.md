### Distribution patterns to test, not copy

1. **Show the app's daily surface:** Motivation's lock-screen previews demonstrate the actual reason to install. Anicca can show one original quote arriving on the lock screen and the tap opening that same quote; attach a unique Apple campaign link where the platform permits it.
2. **Use a concrete emotional moment before the affirmation:** the strongest observed Innertune TikToks start with a timely or emotionally specific scenario, then move through short captioned beats to one action. Use a verified, non-medical, non-metaphysical user problem; do not repeat unsupported claims.
3. **Make cards saveable and shareable:** ThinkUp's cover + three short affirmation/support-line cards explicitly asks to save/share. Build original English cards, then transcreate only after the English version has a measured result.
4. **Treat the CTA as part of the creative:** “screenshot/save”, “comment a keyword”, “link in bio”, and “open the lock screen” are distinct behaviors. Track each through a unique channel/creative ct; account views and likes alone cannot establish store clicks or installs.
5. **Use portfolio distribution:** Monkey Taps' official sites cross-promote I Am and Motivation, while the Innertune site links its app and all major social profiles. This is an owned-distribution system, not proof that any one creative caused its app-store ratings.

**Anicca test candidate:** one original 10–15 second English video: first second states a specific moment (e.g. “Your mind is still replaying that conversation?”), then show the Anicca quote/notification on a phone, then tap and show the same quote in-app, finish with one trackable App Store CTA. This is only a test hypothesis; use one existing slot, create three distinct original posts, and compare views/clicks/ASC first-time downloads. Do not use testimonial, health, financial or guaranteed-outcome claims without evidence.
