# Manager — a research proposal for structural environment design

Early-stage design, October 2026. No demonstrated real-world outcomes yet.

**Mission:** One entity, blessing for all. We are building ambient AI that models its environment, compares structural changes, acts through authorized connections, and learns from measured outcomes. Beneficiaries should not need an account, login, or personal credentials.

**Research question:** Can a system identify changes to supply, access, capacity, or shared rules that improve measured outcomes across different groups, and distinguish those effects from artifacts of its own behavioral model?

**Initial reference:** Two numerical scenarios—service access and shared-resource allocation—using one intervention interface. A behavioral response model produces demands; a separate transition model enforces capacity, budget, time, and resource constraints. Candidate policies are compared against no-change and explicit rule baselines under paired external shocks.

**Evidence:** Versioned data and assumptions; held-out scenarios; distributional outcomes; failed candidates; full costs; prediction errors; independent action readback. Simulation comparison, empirical prediction, and real intervention evaluation are separate stages.

**What we would value:** Criticism of the modeling boundaries, evaluation design, and how to detect effects caused by agent prompts rather than changes in the shared environment. A short written response or research conversation would help refine the protocol.

**Long-term goal:** Improvements in material security, health, time, opportunity, and the conditions of life. These goals do not imply that a prototype has achieved AGI, predicts all behavior, or demonstrates benefits to all living beings.
