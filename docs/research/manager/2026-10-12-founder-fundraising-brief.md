## 製品と使命

Manager — One entity, blessing for all.

**Ambient AI that redesigns shared environments to improve people's lives.**

Manager models populations and systems, compares structural changes, acts within the environment operator's authority, and measures what happened. Beneficiaries do not need an account, app, or chat interface. The explanatory website and public evaluation records make the work visible. The initial research population is human; other living beings require separate ecological models and validation.

Life Manager is the current business-building execution system: **AI that builds businesses and creates wealth.** Manager adds population modeling, environment-level decisions, and independently measured outcomes. One company, shared mission, different intervention units. Legacy Telegram is an execution-system transport, not the new Manager beneficiary experience.

## YCとSpeedrunへ伝える説明

**What are you building?**

We are building Manager, ambient AI that improves people's lives by redesigning the shared environments that shape their opportunities and wellbeing. It models how populations respond to structural changes, selects interventions, executes them with environment operators, and measures outcomes. People benefit without having to use an app.

**What problem?**

People's choices are constrained by access, supply, time, cost, and shared rules. Most AI products advise individuals; changing those shared conditions can improve outcomes for many people at once. Organizations need evidence that a proposed change will help and a reliable way to execute and evaluate it.

**How far along?**

Our existing Life Manager system automates business-building workflows. The founder reports revenue from that system. Manager's population and environment-level system is currently being developed; its architecture, evaluation design, research articles, and talk materials are prepared. No completed Manager implementation or measured population-level wellbeing outcome is claimed in this brief.

The historical approximately USD 1,000 attestation in the September startup context is dated August 26; it is not a refreshed October total. Use the current period, amount, receipts, costs, and human involvement when available. Own-business sales, revenue earned by customers, simulation scores, and field outcomes are different claims.

**Why this founder?**

The founder has master's research experience in behavioral measurement and EEG-based machine learning, including participant-held-out evaluation, and enterprise AI-agent deployment and response-analysis experience. Manager combines that measurement background with execution engineering. Research publications, GPU training, years of production experience, and finished Manager results must be claimed only when actually supported.

**Who pays?**

Initial payer hypothesis: organizations that operate shared services, allocate resources, or need population-response evaluation. Beneficiaries need not be customers. Paid evaluation projects or operator pilots are possible starting products; customer commitments and Manager revenue are not yet established by this brief.

**Why different?**

Aaru and Simile emphasize population and behavioral prediction. AI Economist explores rules and adaptive economic agents. Manager aims to connect grounded prediction to structural intervention, execution, and measured outcomes. This is a proposed integration, not proof that existing companies lack execution features or that Manager is scientifically novel.
