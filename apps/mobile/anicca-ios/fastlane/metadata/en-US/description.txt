Daily affirmation cards for self-love, calm, and confidence.

Choose from 13 support topics, including anxiety, overthinking, loneliness, and sleep. Read a short affirmation, save cards you want to revisit, and choose from eight calming backgrounds. With notifications enabled, Anicca can send gentle reminders related to the topics you selected.

THE PHILOSOPHY

"Anicca" is a Pali word from Buddhist teaching meaning "impermanence" — a reminder that feelings and difficult moments can change. Each affirmation can be a small pause in your day.
