Life Manager Cloud on Maritime

Life Manager Cloud is the hosted mode of Life Manager — one proactive manager for work, money, everyday responsibilities, body, and mind. It runs continuously on a dedicated Maritime VM, and you use it from your phone.

There is one Life Manager Cloud. Maritime is where it is hosted, not a separate product or edition. The manager, the pricing, and the way it works are the same.

Start Life Manager Cloud
How it starts

Google identity only. Nothing else required at signup.

Google sign-in verifies identity only. Signup does not require Calendar or Gmail inbox access, a task prompt, goals, budget, or payout details. Progress and verified results are designed to go to your sign-in email.

How earning works

Verified before it is reported.

The first earning workflow is connected to Capafy. Capafy requires Publisher Certification and publisher confirmation of selected Skills and configuration before each Agent version can go live. Life Manager reports listings and earnings only after Capafy confirms them; earnings are not guaranteed.

Pricing

One plan.

Life Manager Cloud is $29/month after a 7-day free trial. Billing is set up in the web app after Calendar setup; no payment details are required at signup.
