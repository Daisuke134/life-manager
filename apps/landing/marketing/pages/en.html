Why we exist

A once-in-a-universe company.

We are not building a money printer. Not a company for a decade, or even a century. We are building a civilizational company.

We found the answer to life.

If an alien asked what the greatest feat of this universe is, our answer would be simple. About 2,500 years ago, a way to end suffering was discovered: enlightenment, through Vipassana. The answer already exists. Anicca exists to decentralize it to every living being.

Some people believe we do not yet know the answer to life, and that we need more time and more intelligence to ask better questions. We respect that view, but we start from a different place. Nirvana is the deepest behavior change there is: letting go of the craving that keeps us suffering. The path is already known. Our job is not to reinvent it, but to make it faster and easier to reach, for everyone.

That only works with consent and transparency. Anicca helps people change in ways they choose and can see. Never covert manipulation.

Our Values

One Flesh

We are building a company that acts as one body. What one learns, we share with all. Each of us takes responsibility for the whole, working toward the end of suffering.

Learn From the Past

Reinvention is a sin. There is no shame in not knowing. We learn humbly from everyone, and most of all from those who won before us, until we understand how their work succeeds and why. Then we build on what already works. Our ego is never a reason to start from zero.

Zero Person.

No human in the loop. Not even the founder. A civilizational company has to survive for thousands of years, and no human can. So Anicca depends on no single person, no single machine, and no one's house. It lives in the world of bits. It does not give up. It keeps growing.

The founder’s story · Daisuke Narita
“How can we
end suffering?”

I began practicing Vipassana at 18, in 2020, at a retreat in Chiba in my last year of high school. What started as a question about my own suffering grew into a question about suffering in the world.

I procrastinated, and I wanted to understand why. That made me curious about how behavior changes, and how technology could help it change. When large language models arrived, technology could finally take part in behavior change itself. Buddhism, behavior change and technology came together into one idea: the Manager.

In graduate school, I began asking how technology could help. Today, I’m building Anicca to work on that same question at a broader scale: the conditions and environments that shape our lives.

Manager · The entity we are building

Not a product. An entity.
Learning from the whole world to end suffering.

Manager is what I aim to build at Anicca: one manager intended to learn from evidence and act only within delegated authority, improving only when measured outcomes support it. Its beneficiaries should not need a dedicated app or chat; operator oversight, audit, and stop controls remain. The long-term vision is for it to grow from managing one person’s life and work into a caring presence for all living beings, in service of ending suffering. Today we are building and measuring its first components; it has not yet demonstrated reliable human-behavior prediction or real-world intervention effects.
