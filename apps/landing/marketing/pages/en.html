The founder’s story · Daisuke Narita
“How can we
end suffering?”

I began practicing Vipassana around the age of 16. What started as a question about my own suffering grew into a question about suffering in the world.

In graduate school, I began asking how technology could help. Today, I’m building Ninja to work on that same question at a broader scale: the conditions and environments that shape our lives.

Personal practice

Finding Vipassana

Exploring suffering and attention through my own meditation practice.

Graduate research & IACS

Personalized neurofeedback

In graduate school, I studied personalized decoders using brain and pupil data. At the Institute for Advanced Consciousness Studies (IACS), I implemented auditory neurofeedback using 64-channel EEG to turn estimated attention states into sound.

Now & what comes next

From individual feedback to shared conditions

Using synthetic populations and behavioral models to investigate which changes could help, then testing those ideas against real outcomes.

Manager · Vision & progress

Understand the world.
Learn by changing it.

Manager is Ninja’s product for pursuing this mission: observe the world, compare possible changes, act, and learn from real outcomes. We begin with components we can build and measure.

Implemented & measured · Population synthesis

Synthetic people. Intact households.

441,570 households

Using Rhode Island public statistics and PUMS, we generated 1,042,763 synthetic people. The output audit found no membership errors, duplicate IDs, or copied-attribute mismatches.
