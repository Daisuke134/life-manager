The founder’s story · Daisuke Narita
“How can we
end suffering?”

I began practicing Vipassana around the age of 16. What started as a question about my own suffering grew into a question about suffering in the world.

In graduate school, I began asking how technology could help. Today, I’m building Anicca to work on that same question at a broader scale: the conditions and environments that shape our lives.

Graduate research & IACS

Personalized neurofeedback

In graduate school, I studied personalized decoders using brain and pupil data. At the Institute for Advanced Consciousness Studies (IACS), I implemented auditory neurofeedback using 64-channel EEG to turn estimated attention states into sound.

Manager · Digital Buddha · In development

Observe. Act. Learn from reality.

Manager is a Buddhist AI entity we call the Digital Buddha. Its mission is to reduce suffering and enable flourishing for all welfare-bearing beings. We are working toward one coherent decision-making model that operates in the background, without routine chat instructions. Its first bounded capability release is Digital Autonomy v0.1: maintaining owned digital information, independently checking changes, and recovering when something goes wrong.

Digital Buddha names the mission and design of this entity; consciousness, enlightenment and universal capability have not been established. Improvement requires evidence; no change, harm and unknown outcomes remain part of the record.

Model experiments · Engineering evidence

Evaluate advice without inventing human benefit.

An authenticated Ampersand experiment used ten fictional scenarios. The newer frozen provider comparison has not run live. A separate calibration diagnostic concerns one historical bike-rental task. Human-behavior fidelity, causal benefit and provider superiority remain unestablished.

Earlier research · Population synthesis, 18 October 2026. We generated 1,042,763 synthetic people in 441,570 households from Rhode Island public statistics and PUMS. That output audit found no membership, duplicate-ID or copied-attribute mismatches. Population construction does not validate behavioral prediction or reduced suffering.
