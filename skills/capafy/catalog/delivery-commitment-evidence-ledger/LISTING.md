## Title
Delivery Commitment Evidence Ledger

## shortDescription
Map pasted delivery commitments and acceptance wording to supplied project evidence, separating direct support, gaps, plans, and unknowns in a source-linked review ledger.

## welcomeMessage
👋 Paste the delivery commitments, acceptance wording, project updates, and evidence excerpts you want compared. I’ll map only the supplied material, keep plans separate from completed work, and make the unknowns visible.

Example: “Our order form requires a daily status file by 09:00 JST with order IDs and delivery state. A project update says the file design is ready, but no delivered sample is included. Build an evidence ledger and verification queue.”

## detailedDescription
✨ **Review delivery commitments against the material you already have**

Paste excerpts from statements of work, order forms, acceptance notes, project updates, and delivery records.
The Agent creates a traceable ledger that preserves the commitment wording and distinguishes a plan or draft from supplied evidence of delivery.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Extracts each stated commitment, timing, owner, and acceptance criterion from your text.
2. Links supplied updates or artifacts only to the commitment they explicitly address.
3. Labels direct support, partial support, missing material, and unclear links separately.
4. Keeps plans, designs, and claimed completion distinct when your material does not connect them.
5. Produces owner questions and verification steps for the next delivery review.

📦 **Each review includes**

- A source-linked commitment ledger
- An evidence-status table
- A gaps and unknowns register
- A verification queue with owner questions
- A concise delivery-check-in brief

💡 **What makes it different**

- **Delivery-focused:** it compares promised items and acceptance wording with the material you supply.
- **Evidence-bound:** it does not treat a plan, draft, or update as proof of completed delivery without support in your text.
- **Careful by design:** it keeps unknowns visible and does not make legal conclusions or certify completion.

👤 **Built for**

Operations, customer-delivery, implementation, and program teams reviewing their own delivery records.
It uses only pasted material and does not inspect contracts, systems, files, customer messages, approvals, or delivery state outside the excerpts you provide.
Have the accountable owner verify every result before operational or legal use.
