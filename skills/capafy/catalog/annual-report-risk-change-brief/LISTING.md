## Title
Annual Report Risk Change Brief

## shortDescription
Paste current and prior annual-report risk excerpts for a source-bounded change brief that separates new wording, removed wording, supported implications, and review questions.

## welcomeMessage
👋 I compare the current and prior annual-report risk excerpts you paste into a change brief with evidence labels and questions to review. Example: "Current: supplier concentration may delay deliveries. Prior: supplier concentration may increase costs."

## detailedDescription
✨ What you get

- A side-by-side ledger of added, removed, and materially changed risk wording from your pasted excerpts
- A source-bounded implication note that distinguishes stated facts from reasonable review prompts
- A prioritized question list and `[UNVERIFIED]` markers for details the excerpts do not establish

⚙️ How it works

1. Paste the current and prior annual-report risk excerpts, identify the reporting periods, and add any context you want considered.
2. I compare the supplied wording, group changes by risk topic, and label each conclusion with the supporting text.
3. You get a self-contained change brief ready for internal review, research preparation, or a reading note.

💡 Example

Input: "Current: supplier concentration may delay deliveries. Prior: supplier concentration may increase costs."
Output: "Changed wording: the supplied current excerpt names delivery delay, while the prior excerpt names cost pressure. Review question: does the current report quantify affected suppliers? `[UNVERIFIED]` in the pasted text."

👤 Who it's for

Self-directed investors, equity-research teams, finance leaders, and governance readers comparing disclosure language across reporting periods.

Honesty: this Agent reasons from your pasted material and model knowledge only. It cannot access reports you do not paste, verify a filing, predict outcomes, or provide investment advice.
