## Actual output

### Positioning

For 20-somethings who think "I have some savings" is the same as "I have an
emergency fund" — this Short recalculates the real number.

### Title options

1. "Your Emergency Fund Is Probably Too Small"
2. "The $1,000 Emergency Fund Myth"
3. "Why 3 Months of Savings Isn't Enough Anymore"

### Opening hooks

1. **On-screen text:** "Your emergency fund is too small." **Spoken:** "If
   you think $1,000 is an emergency fund, this is your wake-up call." **Type:**
   contrarian.
2. **On-screen text:** "I did the math on this." **Spoken:** "I ran the real
   numbers on what a 24-year-old actually needs saved." **Type:** curiosity.
3. **On-screen text:** "3 months isn't enough." **Spoken:** "The old '3
   months of expenses' rule is outdated for most 24-year-olds." **Type:**
   stakes.
4. **On-screen text:** "This changed my savings goal." **Spoken:** "This one
   recalculation changed how much I keep in savings." **Type:** result.
5. **On-screen text:** "If you're 24, watch this." **Spoken:** "If you're in
   your mid-20s with less than this saved, watch this." **Type:** callout.

### Recommended title + hook

Title 1 ("Your Emergency Fund Is Probably Too Small") paired with Hook 1: the
title states the exact claim the hook opens with, so a viewer who taps from
the feed sees the promise restated immediately — reducing the swipe-away risk
Shorts titles are judged on before autoplay even starts.

### Script with retention beats

| Time | Beat | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Hook | "If you think $1,000 is an emergency fund, this is your wake-up call." On-screen: "Your emergency fund is too small." |
| 0:03–0:14 | Setup | Explain the old "3 months of expenses" rule and why it was built for a different cost-of-living era. |
| 0:14–0:16 | **Retention beat @15s** | Pattern interrupt: cut to a number-breakdown card re-asking "so what's the real number?" — new visual, re-poses the hook's question to hold viewers who are about to swipe. |

### Description, hashtags, tip

Description: "The old emergency-fund rule is outdated for most 24-year-olds.
Here's the real number — and it depends on whether you're renting or not."

bottom third of the frame — YouTube's Shorts subscribe/like overlay sits in
that zone and will cover on-screen text placed too low.
