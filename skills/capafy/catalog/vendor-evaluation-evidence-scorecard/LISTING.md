## welcomeMessage

## detailedDescription
✨ **Turn a vendor shortlist into a checkable decision record**

Bring the proposal excerpts, comparison notes, and criteria your team already has. This Agent organizes that supplied material into a scorecard for a vendor-selection conversation.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Separates supplied statements from missing information.
2. Scores direct and partial support using your scale or a stated 0–2 default.
3. Applies your weights and shows the reason behind every score.
4. Tests non-negotiables separately from the total.
5. Produces follow-up questions and conditional options for the accountable buyer.

📦 **Each review includes**

- A vendor-by-criterion evidence scorecard
- Weighted totals with the stated calculation basis
- A non-negotiable status table
- An evidence-gap ledger and prioritized review agenda
- Conditional options that keep unresolved items visible

💡 **What makes it different**

- **Evidence-bound:** every score traces to supplied text or is marked not supplied.
- **Requirement-aware:** a high total does not hide a missing non-negotiable.
- **Honest:** it uses your pasted material and does not treat unknown vendor details as confirmed.

👤 **Built for**

Procurement leads, operations teams, founders, and product buyers preparing a vendor decision from the material already in their hands. Confirm pricing, terms, security, implementation scope, and final approval with the accountable owner.
