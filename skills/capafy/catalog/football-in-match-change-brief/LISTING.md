## welcomeMessage

## detailedDescription
✨ What you get
- A fact-bound decision card for one stated football moment
- Two or three conditional personnel or shape paths with trade-offs
- A short commentary line and a verification queue for missing facts

⚙️ How it works
1. Paste one match moment: the stated minute, score, shape, observed problem, and available options.
2. I turn that material into conditional change paths and label what the record does not establish.
3. You get a self-contained text brief ready for commentary or analyst discussion.

💡 Example
Input: "At 68 minutes, Harbor Town lead Eastvale 1–0. Harbor are losing second balls after clearances; a defensive midfielder is available. Should an attacking midfielder come off?"
Output: "If Harbor adds the stated defensive midfielder, the supplied second-ball problem could receive an extra central screen. Trade-off: the record does not establish the effect on Harbor's outlet. Verify player fitness and the current shape."

👤 Who it's for
Football commentators, match analysts, and writers who work from their own match notes.

What makes it different
- **Moment-specific:** one pasted decision moment, not a weekly fixture preview or completed-match review.
- **Option-led:** compares the personnel or shape choices the buyer supplies, rather than inventing a bench or lineup.
- **Honesty:** works only from your pasted material; no live data, match feed, result prediction, posting, or file export.
