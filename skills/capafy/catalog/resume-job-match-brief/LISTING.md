## Title
Resume-to-Job Match Brief — Gaps & Bullet Rewrites

## shortDescription
Paste a job posting and your current resume. Get a requirement-by-requirement match table, the gaps to address honestly, and rewritten bullets that use only the experience you supplied. Text output, no invented achievements.

## welcomeMessage
👋 I compare your pasted resume with one pasted job posting and show where you match, where you have gaps, and how to reword your real experience. Example: "Posting: Customer Success Manager, needs renewals, onboarding and Salesforce. Resume bullet: Handled client accounts and trained new users." Paste yours and I will build the match brief.

## detailedDescription
✨ What you get

- A requirement-by-requirement match table: matched, partly matched, or not shown in your resume
- A short list of gaps, with honest ways to address each one
- Rewritten bullets that use only the experience in your pasted resume
- Keywords from the posting that your resume already supports

⚙️ How it works

1. Paste one job posting and your current resume (new posting each application).
2. I split the posting into requirements and check each against your resume text.
3. You get a self-contained text brief: match table, gap list and rewritten bullets.

💡 Example

Input: "Posting: Customer Success Manager — renewals, onboarding, Salesforce. Resume: Handled client accounts and trained new users."
Output: "Onboarding: matched — 'trained new users'. Renewals: not shown in your resume; add a real renewal example if you have one `[ADD: renewal detail]`. Salesforce: not mentioned `[ADD: CRM tool if used]`. Rewrite: 'Managed client accounts and trained new users during onboarding.'"

👤 Who it's for

Job seekers applying to several roles, career changers, and anyone who wants each application to reflect the posting in front of them.

Honesty: this Agent works from the posting and resume you paste and general hiring knowledge. It does not invent experience, submit applications, check any applicant tracking system, or guarantee interviews or offers.
