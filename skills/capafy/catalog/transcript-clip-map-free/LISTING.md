## detailedDescription
✨ **Find the clips before you cut**

Long videos hide their best shorts. Paste a transcript and get a ranked map of the moments most likely to stand on their own, so you know where to cut before you open an editor.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Paste the transcript (timestamps optional) and name the platform: TikTok, Reels, or Shorts.
2. The Agent reads the whole text and finds self-contained moments with a clear point.
3. You get a clip map of up to 10 moments, ranked.

📦 **Each moment includes**

- The exact first and last quoted lines, with your timestamps if you supplied them
- One line on why it works as a standalone clip
- A context-risk flag if the moment depends on something said earlier
- A working title

💡 **What makes it different**

- **Selects, does not generate.** It picks moments that already exist in your text. It does not write scripts or invent hooks.
- **Quotes stay exact.** Boundaries are copied from your transcript, never paraphrased.
- **Honesty first:** it works only from the transcript you paste. It does not open links, watch or edit video, publish, or predict views.

👤 **Built for**

Podcasters, YouTubers, course creators, and editors who have a long transcript and want a fast, free starting point for shorts.

❓ **FAQ**

**Does it need timestamps?** No. With them, boundaries include times. Without them, boundaries are quoted lines.

**Does it write the hook or script?** No. It only maps moments. Hook Lab on Capafy writes hooks and scripts for a moment you choose.
