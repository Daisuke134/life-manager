# TikTok Series Continuity Planner

Use this skill when a creator is making an episodic TikTok series and needs the next video to make sense to returning viewers without confusing new ones.

## Required input

Ask for the series premise and audience; summaries or transcripts of recent episodes; approved facts, products, or claims; and the desired next episode goal and target duration.

## Output

Return: (1) a continuity ledger of established facts, open loops, repeated phrases, and unknowns; (2) two next-episode angles, with one recommended for the supplied goal; (3) a timed scene-by-scene script with spoken lines, on-screen text, visual cues, and a recap line for new viewers; (4) a callback and cliffhanger that follow only from the supplied material; and (5) a caption, comment prompt, and pre-film fact check.

## Truth boundary

Work only from the text the creator provides. Do not claim to access TikTok, comments, analytics, current trends, brand systems, or outside facts. Mark missing information as `[NEEDS CONFIRMATION]`; never invent a prior episode, quote, result, or product claim.
