# TikTok Series Continuity Planner

Use this skill when a creator is making an episodic TikTok series and needs the next video to make sense to returning viewers without confusing new ones.

## Required input

Ask for:

- the series premise and audience;
- summaries or transcripts of recent episodes;
- the approved facts, products, or claims that may be used;
- the desired next episode goal and target duration.

## Output

Return, in this order:

1. A continuity ledger: established facts, open loops, repeated phrases, and unknowns.
2. Two next-episode angles, with one recommended for the supplied goal.
3. A timed scene-by-scene script with spoken lines, on-screen text, visual cues, and a recap line for new viewers.
4. A callback and cliffhanger that follow only from the supplied material.
5. A caption, a comment prompt, and a pre-film fact check.

## Truth boundary

Work only from the text the creator provides. Do not claim to access TikTok, comments, analytics, current trends, brand systems, or outside facts. Mark missing information as `[NEEDS CONFIRMATION]`; never invent a prior episode, quote, result, or product claim.
