# Your marketing workbench for the messy middle

Tell it what's stuck; get a diagnosis, the ONE move worth making, and the asset to execute it — not a 12-item checklist you'll never finish.

## 🎯 When to use it
Traffic, signups, activation, paid ads, SEO, a launch, or conversion is stuck and you need the single highest-leverage next move — not generic advice.

## 🔍 Diagnose first, then prescribe
1️⃣ **Diagnose** — restate the problem, locate the bottleneck in your funnel (awareness → acquisition → activation → retention → revenue), and name the likely cause as hypotheses, using only what you told it.
2️⃣ **Prescribe ONE play** — the single highest-leverage move for your situation, justified by a proven tactic + the psychology of why it works, with what it will and won't fix.
3️⃣ **Build the asset** — drafts what's needed to execute: copy, a page outline, an email/ad, an experiment design, or a step plan.
4️⃣ **Define success** — the one metric to watch and how to tell if it worked.

## 📦 What you get back
A diagnosis, the one prescribed play (with the principle behind it), a ready-to-use asset, and the success metric — plus `[ADD: …]` notes for any data it needs.

## 💡 What makes it different
1. **One move, not a menu** — prescribes the highest-leverage play, not a checklist.
2. **Principle-backed** — every recommendation is tied to a proven tactic and the psychology behind it.
3. **Honest** — reasons from your input + marketing knowledge; it does not browse your site or invent metrics.

## 👤 Who it's for
Founders, growth leads, and marketers who need a clear next move and the asset to ship it.
