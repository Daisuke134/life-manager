## Title
Earnings Call Brief — Pasted Results to Questions

## shortDescription
Paste an earnings-call excerpt, shareholder letter, and your figures for each reporting period; get a source-bounded brief that separates reported facts, management statements, changes, and open questions.

## welcomeMessage
👋 I turn your pasted earnings transcript, shareholder letter, and supplied figures into a clear results brief with evidence labels and review questions. Example: “Northstar Q2: revenue was $42m versus $38m last year; management said renewal timing moved some deals into Q3; gross margin was 61%.”

## detailedDescription
✨ What you get

- A concise reporting-period readout that separates reported figures from management statements
- A fact ledger showing what your pasted record supports and what remains `[UNVERIFIED]`
- Five specific questions for your next research or internal review

⚙️ How it works

1. Paste your earnings-call excerpt, shareholder letter, reporting period, and any figures you want considered.
2. I organize the supplied material into reported facts, stated drivers, changes, and missing information.
3. You get a self-contained text brief with an evidence boundary and review questions ready to use.

💡 Example

Input: “Northstar Q2 revenue was $42m versus $38m last year. Gross margin was 61%. Management said renewal timing moved some deals into Q3.”
Output: “Reported: revenue changed from $38m to $42m in the pasted comparison. Management statement: some deals moved into Q3 because of renewal timing. Open question: the pasted record does not state the value or timing of those deals `[UNVERIFIED]`.”

👤 Who it's for

Self-directed investors, equity-research teams, finance leaders, and investor-relations readers who
need a consistent brief from material they already have.

Honesty: this Agent reasons from your pasted material and model knowledge only. It cannot access
material beyond what you paste, predict results, or give investment advice.
