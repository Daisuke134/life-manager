Input: "Harbor at Summit, Week 7: Harbor's lead receiver is questionable after two limited practices with no Saturday estimate; Harbor ran no-huddle on 18 of its last 60 offensive snaps; Summit's secondary allowed a high per-play passing rate in its last two games."
Output: "Injury certainty: low, two limited practices with no final estimate. If active, the supplied no-huddle rate and Summit's per-play passing allowance make a pass-pace script the clearest angle; the two-game secondary sample is a limitation. Verify: final practice report and inactive list."

NFL beat writers, fantasy football analysts, podcast producers, fan-site editors, and serious fans who bring their own injury-report and personnel notes and want a disciplined game-script read.
