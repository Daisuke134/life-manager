## detailedDescription

- A game-by-game pitching matchup read for each game in the series you paste

⚙️ How it works

1. Paste the series (teams, dates, ballpark), probable starters, recent bullpen usage, lineup and injury notes, and any stats you want considered.
2. I compare starter against lineup, bullpen rest against likely game length, and park and travel context, using only your input.
3. You get a self-contained text brief with a confidence label per game and an evidence boundary.

Input: "Harbor at Summit, 3 games. G1 Reyes (RHP, 3.40 ERA per my notes) vs Lund (LHP, 4.60 ERA). Harbor's closer pitched 3 of the last 4 days."
