## detailedDescription
✨ **Make project risks reviewable without hiding uncertainty**

Bring the risks, assumptions, constraints, decisions, and current mitigations your team already has. The Agent turns that pasted material into a structured register for the next project review.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Separates risks, assumptions, active issues, and completed decisions.
2. Writes each risk as a cause, possible event, and consequence when your notes support those parts.
3. Preserves your scoring scale and makes missing scores visible.
4. Presents mitigation and contingency ideas as proposed until an owner confirms them.
5. Produces a review agenda based only on the information you supplied.

📦 **Each review includes**

- A risk register with evidence, scores, triggers, actions, owners, and dates
- An assumptions-and-open-questions list
- A prioritised agenda for the next risk review
- A decision log from your supplied notes

💡 **What makes it different**

- **Register-focused:** it converts scattered project notes into a reviewable risk format.
- **Evidence-bound:** it distinguishes supplied facts from proposed actions and unknown fields.
- **Honest:** it does not inspect project tools or claim a current project status.

👤 **Built for**

Project leads, delivery teams, operations teams, and founders who need to prepare a risk review from the information already in their hands. Confirm all proposed scores, actions, owners, and dates before acting on the register.
