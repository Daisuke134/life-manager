## Title
Sales Account Plan Deck

## shortDescription
Turn pasted deal and account facts into an evidence-linked account-plan deck outline. Keep buyer statements, supplied proof, seller hypotheses, and missing evidence separate before your team decides what to prepare for the next meeting.

## welcomeMessage
👋 Paste the account context, buyer roles, discovery notes, proof, objections, risks, next meeting, and the internal decision needed. I’ll turn only that material into an evidence-linked account-plan deck outline.

Example: “Northstar’s operations lead wants fewer manual handoffs. We have two discovery notes, one case-study metric, a security question, and a meeting next Tuesday. Decide what evidence the account team needs before proposing a pilot.”

## detailedDescription
✨ **A decision deck for the account meeting you actually have**

Paste your account context, discovery notes, buyer roles, supplied proof, objections,
risks, next meeting, and the decision your team needs to make. This Agent turns only
that material into a concise account-plan deck outline for your own presentation tool.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Paste the account facts, buyer statements, goals, proof, risks, and requested decision.
2. It labels each material statement `SUPPLIED`, `BUYER-REPORTED`, `HYPOTHESIS`, or `[UNVERIFIED]`.
3. It maps stakeholder roles and account goals only to the evidence you provide.
4. It builds a seven-slide decision outline, an evidence matrix, and a next-meeting action register.

📦 **What you receive**

| Deliverable | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Account-plan brief | States the supplied deal context, decision, evidence, and unknowns |
| Evidence ledger | Keeps proof, buyer statements, hypotheses, and gaps separate |
| Seven-slide outline | Provides a title, takeaway, evidence, and visual suggestion for each slide |
| Stakeholder matrix | Shows only the supplied roles, priorities, influence, and evidence |
| Action register | Records decision options and uses supplied owners and dates when available |

💡 **What makes it different**

- It is for account-plan preparation, not a generic deck or a customer escalation.
- It connects every proposed meeting move to supplied buyer evidence or marks it as a hypothesis.
- It turns recurring discovery material into a comparable decision format without assuming a deal outcome.
- **Honesty first:** it reasons from your pasted material and model knowledge only. It does not browse, retrieve records, access a CRM, send messages, or promise a win.

👤 **Built for**

Account executives, sales engineers, customer-growth teams, and sales leaders preparing a focused internal account-plan review.
