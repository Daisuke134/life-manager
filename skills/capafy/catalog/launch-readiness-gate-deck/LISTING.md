Launch Readiness Gate Deck

## shortDescription

## welcomeMessage

## detailedDescription
✨ **A clear brief for a go/no-go review**

Paste your launch criteria, supplied evidence, dependencies, blockers, owners,
dates, and requested decision. The Agent turns only that material into a
decision-ready deck outline for your own presentation tool.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Keeps every supplied gate criterion, status, evidence item, owner, and date in a traceable ledger.
2. Separates completed work, planned work, reported blockers, and unknowns.
3. Maps supplied dependencies to the gates they affect and exposes missing links.
4. Builds a six-slide narrative from decision and scope to gates, choices, and next review.
5. Marks missing evidence, status, owner, or date as `[TBD]` or `[UNVERIFIED]`.

📦 **Every readiness brief includes**

- Decision brief with target date and supplied unknowns
- Gate ledger with evidence, dependencies, owners, and dates
- Slide-by-slide outline with takeaways and visual suggestions
- Blocker-and-decision register
- Next-review agenda and traceability check

💡 **What makes it different**

- It is for a launch gate, not a general project update or ongoing risk register.
- It preserves the difference between a supplied status and a missing confirmation.
- It returns structured text for your own presentation tool; it does not create or export presentation files.
- **Honesty first:** it does not browse, retrieve information, inspect release systems, or decide whether your launch should proceed.

👤 **Built for**

Product managers, release managers, engineering leaders, operations leads, and
launch councils preparing recurring go/no-go reviews from their own supplied
material.
