## detailedDescription
# TikTok Storytime Script

A personal story gets attention only when viewers can follow the change: what was normal, what went wrong, the moment it shifted, and what you learned. Paste the facts of a real experience and receive a filmable storytime package—not generic content advice.

## What it does

It organizes your supplied facts into a compact narrative spine, then writes a scene-by-scene spoken script. Every scene includes a visual you can capture simply, on-screen text, and timing. It protects uncertainty: it flags missing facts instead of making up a quote, result, date, or outcome.

## Best for

- Founder lessons and behind-the-scenes decisions
- Customer-service moments, with identifying details removed
- Career mistakes, pivots, and practical lessons
- Before-and-after process stories based on facts you supply

## How it works

1. **Truth boundary** — identifies supplied facts, optional anonymization, and anything that must not be inferred.
2. **Narrative spine** — sets the ordinary situation, tension, turning point, result, and takeaway.
3. **Opening choices** — offers two honest opening lines and recommends one based on the supplied story.
4. **Scene script** — writes spoken lines, visual/shot cues, on-screen text, and approximate timing for each beat.
5. **Safe finish** — provides a caption, 4–6 relevant hashtags, and a low-pressure CTA that matches the story.

## What you get back

- A fact boundary and privacy check
- Two opening choices, one marked RECOMMENDED
- A scene-by-scene script table: time, spoken line, shot, on-screen text
- A reveal and takeaway that do not overstate the evidence
- Caption, hashtags, and one filming note

## What makes it different

This is for story-led videos, not product explainers or trend scripts. It designs the emotional sequence and the footage plan around one real event. It also never claims a result you did not give it.

**Can it use private details?** Only if you provide them. Tell it what to anonymize and it will use neutral placeholders.

**Does it access TikTok or research other stories?** No. It works only from your text and does not connect to accounts.

**Which plan fits?** Day is for one story; week is for a batch; month and year fit regular story-led publishing.
