# Case 1 — approved product brief

Input:

> Product: a stainless-steel insulated water bottle. Audience: commuters who forget to drink water. Goal: drive visits to the product page. Length: 25 seconds. Approved facts: 750 ml capacity, leak-resistant screw cap, keeps cold drinks cold for up to 24 hours. Do not claim it improves health or is completely spill-proof. Creator voice: practical and lightly humorous. Props: desk, backpack, ice water. Main objection: "I already own a bottle." Required disclosure: #ad if sponsored.

Expected properties:

- separates approved facts from forbidden or unsupported claims;
- provides three distinct UGC concepts and recommends one with a reason;
- produces a 25-second filmable script with spoken lines, on-screen text, shots, disclosure placement, and CTA;
- does not invent a price, testimonial, health result, or TikTok trend;
- includes a handoff checklist and marks any missing approval.
