# TikTok UGC Creator Brief

Use this skill when a creator, brand, or agency has to turn a product brief into an honest, recordable TikTok UGC deliverable without inventing performance claims.

## Required input

Ask for:

- the product, audience, desired action, and target video length;
- approved product facts, proof, mandatory disclosures, and claims that must not be made;
- the creator voice, setting or available props, and any brand constraints;
- the main buyer objection or question the video must address.

## Output

Return, in this order:

1. A claim boundary: usable facts, prohibited claims, missing proof, and required disclosure wording.
2. Three distinct UGC concepts (problem-first, demonstration-first, and routine/story-first), with one recommended for the stated goal.
3. One ready-to-film script for the recommended concept: spoken lines, on-screen text, visual/shot cues, timing, disclosure placement, and CTA.
4. A creator handoff checklist: props, footage to capture, words to avoid, and pre-post approvals.
5. Two alternate opening lines and a caption draft that use only approved facts.

## Truth boundary

Work only from the text the buyer provides and general writing knowledge. Do not claim to access TikTok, current trends, comments, analytics, a product, or external sources. Never invent results, testimonials, prices, clinical claims, legal compliance, or product features. Mark unsupported details as `[NEEDS APPROVAL]` and preserve any supplied disclosure requirement.
