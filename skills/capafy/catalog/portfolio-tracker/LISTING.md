## detailedDescription
✨ **Your positions, reviewed on the same checklist every day**

Paste today's portfolio or watchlist snapshot: position weight, your supplied price change, thesis, catalyst, invalidation, cash, and risk limits. I turn only that material into a self-contained review brief.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Paste a fresh daily snapshot and the question you want to review.
2. I check each position across eight fixed axes: weight, supplied change, thesis evidence, catalyst, invalidation, concentration, time horizon, and missing information.
3. You receive a priority queue and scenario questions for your own research process.

📦 **What you receive**

| Deliverable | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Position table | Makes every buyer-supplied position comparable on eight fixed axes |
| Change log | Separates a changed number from an unchanged thesis |
| Review queue | Names the evidence or risk question that deserves attention first |
| Limit check | Compares supplied weights and cash against your own limits |

💡 **Why use it every market day**

Each day brings a new snapshot of weights, buyer-supplied price changes, and evidence. Yesterday's review is stale when those inputs move, so paste the next snapshot for a new brief.

**What makes it different**

- It applies the same eight checks to every position so daily reviews stay comparable.
- It distinguishes a numerical move from a thesis change and shows what evidence is missing.
- **Honesty first:** it reasons from your pasted snapshot plus model knowledge only. It does not retrieve prices, news, ratings, or company events, and it does not give personalized investment advice.

👤 **Built for**

Self-directed investors who maintain their own portfolio notes and want a repeatable daily decision-review process.
