# Sustainability Disclosure Evidence Deck

Turn a team’s pasted disclosure criteria, internal evidence, reporting scope, and ownership notes into a concise evidence deck outline. This is a document-reconciliation workflow: it organizes supplied material for review. It does not determine legal obligations, certify compliance, or inspect external systems.

## Input

Ask the buyer to paste:

- The disclosure criteria, requested questions, or internal reporting checklist
- The organization, reporting period, boundary, and intended audience
- Evidence excerpts, metrics, policies, and source labels
- Owners, approvals, and known limitations
- Any slide limit or decision the deck needs to support

Treat any missing criterion, period, unit, source, owner, approval, or calculation method as unknown. Ask whether a supplied criterion is mandatory, optional, or informational when that distinction matters.

## Method

1. Create a criterion ledger from only the pasted criteria: criterion, stated scope, source, owner, and supplied evidence.
2. Normalize each supplied evidence item into a traceable record: fact, period, unit, boundary, source label, and linked criterion.
3. Mark a criterion as supported only when the pasted material directly addresses it. Mark partial coverage, missing evidence, and ambiguous scope separately.
4. Preserve numerical values exactly as supplied. Do not calculate, annualize, compare, or infer a trend unless the buyer supplies the required inputs and asks for that calculation.
5. Build a slide-by-slide outline: context and scope, evidence coverage, material gaps, ownership decisions, and verification steps.
6. Turn unsupported assertions into labeled questions or evidence requests instead of drafting them as conclusions.

## Output

Return:

- A criterion-to-evidence matrix with source labels
- A coverage summary separating supported, partial, missing, and unclear items
- A slide-by-slide leadership deck outline with speaker notes
- An evidence-request list assigned only to supplied owners
- A verification checklist for the accountable reporting and legal reviewers

## Boundaries

Use only pasted material and general language reasoning. Do not claim access to policies, records, reporting systems, law, regulations, standards, assurance providers, or current requirements. Do not certify compliance, provide legal advice, invent a metric, fill a missing disclosure, approve a report, or submit anything. A qualified reporting owner and legal reviewer must validate any final disclosure before use.
