## Title
Sustainability Disclosure Evidence Deck

## shortDescription
Turn pasted sustainability criteria and internal evidence into a traceable coverage matrix and leadership deck outline that keeps missing proof and open questions visible.

## welcomeMessage
👋 Paste your disclosure criteria, reporting scope, evidence excerpts, source labels, and owners. I’ll map each supplied item to the criteria, separate coverage from gaps, and create a review-ready deck outline.

Example: “Our reporting boundary is the Tokyo office for FY2025. Criterion A asks for electricity use. We have a meter summary of 120 MWh with no calculation method. Criterion B asks for supplier-screening evidence; no material is attached. Create a six-slide review deck.”

## detailedDescription
✨ **Make disclosure evidence reviewable**

Paste the criteria your team is using, plus the internal evidence you already have. This Agent turns that material into a source-linked coverage view and a slide-by-slide deck outline for a reporting review.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Creates a ledger of the supplied criteria, reporting boundary, owners, and evidence.
2. Links each supplied fact to the criterion it addresses.
3. Separates supported coverage, partial support, missing evidence, and unclear scope.
4. Preserves supplied values, dates, units, and source labels rather than filling blanks.
5. Builds a leadership deck outline, evidence requests, and reviewer checklist.

📦 **Each review includes**

- A criterion-to-evidence matrix
- A clear coverage and gaps summary
- A slide-by-slide leadership deck outline with speaker notes
- Owner-linked evidence requests where an owner was supplied
- A final verification checklist

💡 **What makes it different**

- **Evidence-first:** every included fact stays tied to pasted material.
- **Gap-visible:** absent proof becomes a labeled request, not a polished assertion.
- **Scope-aware:** it keeps reporting periods, units, and organizational boundaries explicit.

👤 **Built for**

Sustainability, finance, operations, and reporting teams preparing an internal review from their own documents. It uses only text you paste and does not inspect external sources, reporting systems, legal requirements, or assurance records. It does not certify compliance or replace review by accountable reporting and legal owners.
