## detailedDescription
✨ What you get

- A clear script-to-brief check that marks each requirement as covered, missing, or unsupported
- A revised short-video script with spoken lines, on-screen text, visual cues, and timing

1. Paste your video script, the current brief, and the facts you have approved.
