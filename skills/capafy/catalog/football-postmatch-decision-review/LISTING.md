## detailedDescription
✨ What you get

- A chronological decision timeline that separates supplied facts from interpretation
- Up to three evidence-linked turning points with uncertainty stated beside each one
- Three next-session priorities and a verification queue for your own review process

⚙️ How it works

1. Paste a completed match's event log, score, tactical decisions, and staff observations (new each time — the match and evidence change).
2. I organize the supplied material into decision moments, observed consequences, and bounded interpretations.
3. You get a self-contained text review ready for a staff debrief, training plan, or post-match explainer.

Input: “Harbor 1–2 Vale. At 62' we changed to 4-4-2 for more box presence; at 68' Vale scored from a wide overload; at 74' we made two crosses without a shot.”

Tactical link: medium confidence; the supplied material does not establish individual assignment errors. Next action: defensive coach reviews wide-cover roles before the next session.”
