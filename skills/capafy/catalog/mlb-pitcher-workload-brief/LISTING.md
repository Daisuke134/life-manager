## Title
MLB Pitcher Workload Brief

## shortDescription
Paste a starter's recent outing log, rest notes, and editorial question for a source-bounded workload brief, decision thresholds, and coverage-ready narrative.

## welcomeMessage
👋 I turn your pasted MLB starter workload notes into a disciplined workload brief. Example: "Harbor's RHP Reyes threw 96 pitches six days ago, 82 pitches one day before that, and left his last outing after five innings; no injury report is supplied. Draft a cautious preview of his next start."

## detailedDescription
✨ What you get

- A starter-workload ledger that separates the outing record you paste from interpretation
- Conditional workload thresholds for a preview or broadcast discussion, with the fact that would change each one
- A coverage-ready narrative and verification queue for your own reporting

⚙️ How it works

1. Paste one pitcher's recent outing log, rest days, stated workload notes, and the next-start question (new each time — the pitcher record and planned start change).
2. I organize the supplied record into documented workload signals, cautious interpretations, and conditional editorial thresholds.
3. You get a self-contained text brief ready to check against your own record before using it.

💡 Example

Input: "Reyes threw 96 pitches six days ago, 82 pitches one day before that, and left his latest start after five innings; no reason is supplied."
Output: "The supplied 96-pitch and 82-pitch outings establish recent workload context, but the exit reason and current plan are [UNVERIFIED]. Frame any early-hook discussion as conditional until those facts are confirmed."

👤 Who it's for

Baseball writers, podcast producers, beat reporters, and serious fans who keep their own pitching notes and want a disciplined workload discussion.

What makes it different

- **Workload-record input:** it reads supplied outing logs and rest notes, not a batting order, completed-game turning point, or one in-game tactical choice.
- **Threshold output:** it produces a workload ledger, conditional editorial thresholds, and a narrative—not a prediction, score forecast, or wager.
- **Honesty first:** it reasons from your pasted material and model knowledge only. It does not access a live game feed, retrieve pitch counts, rotations, injury reports, or availability updates; it does not place wagers or promise a result.
