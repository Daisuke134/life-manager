## Title
Podcast Clip Hook Lab — Transcript Openers

## shortDescription
Turn a pasted podcast excerpt into a source-bounded clip diagnosis, five distinct opening hooks, caption openings, and a 0–3-second text blueprint. It preserves supplied quotes, flags missing context `[ADD: ...]`, and does not edit audio or video, publish clips, or predict performance.

## welcomeMessage
👋 Paste the episode context, audience, transcript excerpt, approved quote, and CTA. I’ll build source-bounded clip hooks, caption openings, and a first-three-seconds text blueprint.

Example: “Audience: new managers. Speaker: Maya. Excerpt: ‘Your first one-on-one is not a status meeting.’ CTA: ‘Hear the full conversation.’”

## detailedDescription
✨ **A stronger opener before the clip is cut**

Paste an episode brief, transcript excerpt, or selected quote, plus the intended audience,
speaker context, and CTA. The Agent turns only that supplied material into a text-first
opening package for a podcast social clip.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Separates supplied context and exact quotes from assumptions, marking missing material `[ADD: ...]`.
2. Diagnoses the excerpt's usable phrase, tension, takeaway, and risk of misleading truncation.
3. Writes five distinct hook structures rather than five minor rewrites of one line.
4. Builds a 0–3-second spoken-text, on-screen-text, and described editorial blueprint.
5. Adds caption openings, the supplied CTA, and a context-and-claim check.

📦 **Every excerpt returns**

- An evidence boundary and excerpt diagnosis
- Five differentiated hooks grounded in supplied wording or a supported paraphrase
- A second-by-second spoken, text, and described editorial blueprint
- Caption openings and the supplied CTA
- An attribution, context, and missing-proof check

💡 **What makes it different**

- It focuses on opening one podcast social clip, not a full episode outline or a generic social calendar.
- It keeps direct quotes, paraphrases, speaker context, and editorial cues separate so misleading truncation is visible.
- **Honesty first:** it works from your pasted material and model reasoning only. It does not browse, inspect media, edit or render audio/video, publish a clip, access metrics, or promise listens, views, or shares.

👤 **Built for**

Podcast producers, hosts, editors, social managers, and agencies who need a repeatable,
context-aware opening package before a clip is cut.
