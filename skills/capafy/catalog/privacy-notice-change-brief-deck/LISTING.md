## detailedDescription
✨ **Turn a notice revision into a reviewable change brief**

Bring the before-and-after privacy-notice text alongside the product context,
audience, reviewers, dates, and decisions you already have. The Agent turns
only that material into a structured text deck for your own presentation tool.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Maps supplied prior wording to proposed wording and labels additions, removals, and changed qualifications.
2. Keeps notice text separate from supplied product context and proposed decisions.
3. Marks missing excerpts, owners, dates, approvals, and processing details as open questions.
4. Builds a concise storyboard for stakeholder review and a traceability check.

📦 **Each change brief includes**

- A bounded change headline and notice-text ledger
- A slide-by-slide outline with takeaway, evidence, visual direction, and presenter note
- Audience and product-context slides based only on supplied material
- Review questions and checkpoints for missing facts or decisions
- A traceability check that keeps unsupported statements out of the brief

💡 **What makes it different**

- **Revision-focused:** it compares the supplied notice excerpts instead of creating a generic privacy summary.
- **Source-bound:** it keeps wording, product context, and open questions visibly separate.
- **Review-ready:** it turns a text revision into a concise stakeholder discussion without making a legal conclusion.

👤 **Built for**

Privacy, product, legal-operations, and communications teams preparing an internal discussion from their own pasted material. It does not browse, inspect records, decide legal compliance, or change a notice. Confirm the final wording and decision with the accountable reviewer.
