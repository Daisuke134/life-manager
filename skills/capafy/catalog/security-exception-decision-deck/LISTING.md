## Title
Security Exception Decision Deck

## shortDescription
Turn pasted exception requests and control context into a source-linked decision deck that separates stated facts, proposed measures, missing evidence, and reviewer questions.

## welcomeMessage
👋 Paste the exception request, relevant control text, risk notes, compensating measures, and owners. I’ll create a decision deck from only that material, keeping unsupported approval or compliance claims out of the review.

Example: “A team requests a 90-day exception to device encryption for two lab laptops. The supplied standard requires encryption for company-managed endpoints. The team proposes locked storage and weekly inventory. No risk owner or approval rule is supplied. Build the review deck without deciding the exception.”

## detailedDescription
✨ **Prepare an exception decision from the material you already have**

Paste a security-exception request alongside the control context, risk notes, proposed measures, dates, and owner comments. The Agent turns the supplied text into a structured deck for the reviewer while keeping missing evidence and approval conditions visible.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. States the requested decision and affected scope from your supplied text.
2. Maps supplied control requirements to the exception rationale and measures.
3. Separates measures described as operating from measures that are only proposed.
4. Shows missing owners, expiry details, risk evidence, and approval criteria as questions.
5. Produces source-linked slides and a confirmation checklist for the accountable reviewer.

📦 **Each decision deck includes**

- A decision summary and supplied scope
- A control-and-exception map with source references
- A risk and compensating-measure table
- Expiry, review, and approval checkpoints based on supplied dates
- An open-questions slide for unsupported or incomplete details
- A reviewer checklist before any decision is recorded

💡 **What makes it different**

- **Exception-specific:** it structures a control deviation for a decision, instead of making a general risk summary.
- **Source-bound:** it ties every factual deck statement to material you pasted.
- **Approval-aware:** it keeps proposed measures and missing approval conditions visible rather than treating them as completed.

👤 **Built for**

Security, risk, IT, and engineering teams preparing an internal exception review from their own pasted material. It uses only supplied text; it does not inspect systems, policies, tickets, audit records, or approval history. Confirm every final statement and decision with the accountable owner.
