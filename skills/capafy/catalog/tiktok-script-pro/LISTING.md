## detailedDescription
# TikTok Script Pro

Paste a rough idea. Get a finished short-video script you can film today — hook-first, timed, and ready to record. Not tips about scripting. The script itself.

## ✨ What it does
It takes a topic, trend, product, or half-formed thought and returns a complete shooting script: three first-three-second hook options, a spoken voiceover written the way people actually talk, on-screen text overlays, a visual cue for every beat, rough timing, and a caption with hashtags. One paste in, one film-ready script out.

## 🎯 When to use / Best For
- **You have a topic but no script** — hand over the idea, get back hooks, voiceover, overlays, and shots.
- **Your opening is weak** — get three engineered first-three-second hooks and the reason the recommended one works.
- **You want to ride a trend** — turn a trend or sound into a concrete short-video angle.
- **You need it filmable** — every line comes with a shot and on-screen text, not just words.
- **You post across platforms** — works for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.

## ⚙️ How it works
1️⃣ **Angle** — it names the single sharpest thing the video will say.
2️⃣ **Hooks** — three scroll-stopping openers for the first ~3 seconds; the strongest is marked RECOMMENDED.
3️⃣ **Script** — HOOK → BUILD → PAYOFF → CTA, with short speakable voiceover.
4️⃣ **Production layers** — on-screen text, visual/B-roll cue, and timing for every beat.
5️⃣ **Posting** — a caption, 4–6 hashtags, and one timing or format tip.

## 📦 What you get back
Every run returns one finished script package: hook options, a timed script table, a caption with hashtags, and a filming tip.

## 💡 What makes it different
1. **Hook-first, always** — the first three seconds decide everything, so that's where it starts.
2. **Film-ready, not advice** — you get the words, the text overlays, and the shots, timed to length.
3. **Speakable voiceover** — written to be said out loud, not read off a page.

## 👤 Who it's for
Creators, founders, marketers, and social media teams who need to turn ideas into posted videos fast.
