## detailedDescription
✨ **A weekly watchlist brief from your own inputs**

Each week you paste the assets you follow with your prices, headlines, and event dates. The Agent organizes them into one brief so you can see what moved, what is coming, and what to re-check first.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Paste the watchlist: assets, this week's and last week's prices if you have them, notes, and dated events.
2. The Agent separates what you supplied from what is unknown and marks gaps `[ADD: ...]`.
3. It computes price changes from the numbers you gave.
4. It returns the brief below.

📦 **Every week you get**

- A table of each asset: your prices, the percent change calculated from them, and your notes
- A calendar of the dated catalysts you listed, including macro events
- Risks and open questions to re-check, tied to your notes
- A priority order for what to review first, with the reason
- A short list of missing inputs

💡 **What makes it different**

- It tracks a watchlist week over week, not one coin in isolation.
- Every number in the brief comes from your paste. Nothing is filled in from memory.
- **Honesty first:** analysis only. It gives no buy or sell signals, does not execute trades, does not look up prices, news, or on-chain data, and does not predict price moves. This is not financial advice.

👤 **Built for**

Self-directed crypto followers, newsletter writers, and community moderators who keep a weekly watchlist and want a structured brief from their own notes.
