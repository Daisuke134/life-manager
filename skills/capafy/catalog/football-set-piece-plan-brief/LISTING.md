## detailedDescription
✨ What you get

- A supplied-evidence ledger separating opponent notes, your constraints, and unknowns
- A defensive or attacking set-piece rehearsal plan with role responsibilities and communication calls
- A short confirmation queue that makes unproved assumptions visible before match day

⚙️ How it works

1. Paste your opponent's set-piece notes, your available-player constraints, the restart type, and training objective (new each time — the opponent, personnel, and task change).
2. I organize the supplied patterns into a rehearsal sequence, role checklist, contingency branches, and clearly labelled unknowns.
3. You get a self-contained text brief ready to adapt in your own coaching session.

💡 Example

Input: "Vale use an inswinging right-corner toward a back-post screen; our tallest centre-back is unavailable and our goalkeeper prefers a clear central lane."
Output: "Rehearse a protected central lane with a designated back-post tracker. The note supports preparation for the described screen, but Vale's current taker and personnel are [UNVERIFIED]; confirm them before assigning a final matchup."

👤 Who it's for

Football coaches, set-piece analysts, academy staff, and video analysts who keep their own opposition and squad notes.

What makes it different

- **Set-piece input:** it uses buyer-pasted corner, free-kick, throw-in, or penalty notes plus squad constraints—not a whole fixture slate, completed-match timeline, or scoreline forecast.
- **Rehearsal output:** it creates a training sequence, role checklist, communication calls, and confirmation queue—not a result prediction or betting view.
- **Honesty first:** it reasons from your pasted material and model knowledge only. It does not access live match feeds, retrieve opponent statistics, know current lineups, post instructions, or promise a match outcome.
