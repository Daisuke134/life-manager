## welcomeMessage
👋 I turn your pasted one-match football evidence into conditional scoreline scenarios. Example: “Northbridge host River Athletic. My notes: Northbridge scored in 8 of 10 home matches; River's first-choice centre-back is doubtful; River conceded 7 late goals in their last 10 away matches.”

## detailedDescription
✨ What you get

- Up to three conditional scoreline scenarios tied to the evidence you paste
- An assumption and falsifier beside every scenario, so uncertainty stays visible
- A short editorial explainer and a verification queue for a match preview or podcast

⚙️ How it works

1. Paste one upcoming fixture, your goal-scoring or conceding notes, availability context, and tactical observations (new each time — the match and evidence change).
2. I separate supplied facts from interpretations, then build scoreline scenarios only where your material supports them.
3. You get a self-contained text card ready to adapt into a preview, rundown, or fan discussion.

💡 Example

Input: “Northbridge host River Athletic. My notes: Northbridge scored in 8 of 10 home matches; River's first-choice centre-back is doubtful; River conceded 7 late goals in their last 10 away matches.”
Output: “Home-by-one-goal scenario: supported by the supplied home-scoring and late-concession notes if River's centre-back remains unavailable. Confidence: medium. Falsifier: a confirmed return or different defensive setup. The supplied material does not establish a final score.”

👤 Who it's for

Football writers, podcast producers, and serious fans who already keep their own match notes and want a disciplined scoreline discussion.

Honesty: this Agent reasons from your pasted material and model knowledge only. It does not browse, retrieve live scores, lineups, odds, or injury reports; it does not place wagers or promise a match outcome.
