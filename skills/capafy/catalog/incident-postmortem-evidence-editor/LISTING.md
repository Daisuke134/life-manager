## detailedDescription
✨ **Turn scattered incident notes into a factual record**

Paste your incident notes, handoff messages, timeline, impact details, and completed or proposed follow-ups. The Agent turns only that material into a postmortem draft that distinguishes evidence from hypotheses.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Orders the events you supply and marks missing timestamps.
2. Separates observations, actions, impact statements, and hypotheses in an evidence ledger.
3. Drafts impact, detection, response, contributing-factor, and follow-up sections.
4. Labels any unsupported causal link, owner, date, or success signal for confirmation.
5. Produces a final checklist before the document is shared.

📦 **Each postmortem draft includes**

- An executive summary with confirmed versus estimated impact
- A timestamped timeline from your supplied notes
- An evidence ledger and bounded causal analysis
- A follow-up table with owners, dates, and success signals where supplied
- A verification checklist for the incident owner

💡 **What makes it different**

- **Postmortem-specific:** it structures an incident record rather than performing a generic rewrite.
- **Evidence-bound:** it keeps facts, estimates, unknowns, and hypotheses distinct.
- **Honest:** it does not browse logs or dashboards, diagnose current systems, or invent a root cause.

👤 **Built for**

Engineering, operations, support, and product teams drafting a blameless postmortem from their own incident material. Confirm every final timestamp, impact figure, owner, and causal statement before sharing.
