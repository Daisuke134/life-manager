## Title
Peer Review Response Editor

## shortDescription
Turn pasted reviewer comments, manuscript excerpts, and real revision evidence into a respectful point-by-point response draft, revision plan, and honesty check without inventing studies, citations, or changes.

## welcomeMessage
👋 Paste the decision letter, reviewer comments, relevant manuscript excerpts, and changes you have actually made. I’ll draft a point-by-point response and flag every evidence gap.

Example: “Reviewer 1 asks for a sensitivity analysis. We only ran the primary analysis and have not added new data. Draft an honest response and revision plan.”

## detailedDescription
✨ **Turn difficult reviewer feedback into an evidence-bound response**

Paste the decision letter, each reviewer comment, relevant manuscript excerpts, changes already made, and constraints. The Agent organizes the feedback and drafts a clear response that stays within the evidence you provide.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Separates each comment into an answerable item.
2. Labels whether the proposed response is supported, needs evidence, unclear, or should decline with rationale.
3. Drafts a respectful point-by-point response.
4. Connects each answer to a manuscript location or marks it `[TBD]`.
5. Builds a revision checklist and a final honesty check.

📦 **Each review round includes**

- Decision summary and submission constraints
- Numbered response-letter draft
- Revision plan with manuscript locations, actions, and evidence gaps
- Optional editor cover-note draft when you supplied the required facts
- Unsupported-claim and `[TBD]` list for author confirmation

💡 **What makes it different**

- It keeps reviewer responses traceable to your pasted evidence.
- It makes evidence gaps visible before they become overclaims.
- It never invents experiments, data, citations, ethics approvals, page numbers, completed changes, or acceptance odds.

👤 **Built for**

Researchers, graduate students, research teams, and academic editors preparing a revision after peer-review feedback.
