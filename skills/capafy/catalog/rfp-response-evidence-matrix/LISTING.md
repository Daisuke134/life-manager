## Title
RFP Response Evidence Matrix

## shortDescription
Map each pasted RFP requirement to supplied company evidence, a cautious draft response, and the exact gaps or verification questions that remain.

## welcomeMessage
👋 Paste the RFP or questionnaire plus the company material you want used. I’ll build a requirement-by-requirement evidence matrix, keep unsupported claims out of the draft, and create a verification queue for the owner.

Example: “The buyer asks for SSO, a 99.9% service commitment, and a 30-day rollout. Our supplied product brief says SAML SSO is available. Our implementation note says typical rollout is four to six weeks. Build a response matrix without filling in the service commitment.”

## detailedDescription
✨ **Build a response plan from the material you already have**

Paste an RFP, procurement questionnaire, or vendor request alongside product, security, implementation, pricing, or policy material. The Agent turns the supplied text into a requirement matrix that shows what is supported, what is incomplete, and what needs an owner’s confirmation.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Breaks the request into individually answerable requirements.
2. Connects each requirement only to relevant material you supplied.
3. Labels each row as supported, partially supported, a gap, a question, or out of scope.
4. Drafts concise wording for supported portions.
5. Moves missing facts and approval-dependent statements into a verification queue.

📦 **Each response plan includes**

- A requirement-and-evidence matrix
- Cautious draft responses with source labels
- A red-flag list for unsupported commitments and ambiguous requests
- A prioritized verification queue for the accountable owner
- A readiness summary that does not decide whether to submit

💡 **What makes it different**

- **Requirement-first:** it maps each buyer request separately instead of producing a generic proposal.
- **Evidence-bound:** it distinguishes supplied support from missing proof.
- **Review-ready:** it gives owners focused questions before they approve final wording.

👤 **Built for**

Sales operations, solution consultants, founders, and procurement-response teams working from their own pasted material. It uses only supplied text; it does not inspect company systems, files, legal terms, or buyer portals. Confirm every final statement with the accountable owner before use.
