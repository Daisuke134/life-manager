## detailedDescription
✨ **Prepare the moments that decide tight matches**

Bring your own match notes, opponent observations, likely formations, player-role details, and tactical objective. Soccer Set-Piece Matchup Brief turns that material into a structured plan for attacking and defending dead-ball situations.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Paste the facts you have about both sides and the match context.
2. State whether you want to protect a lead, create an early chance, or target a specific weakness.
3. Receive an attacking-corner plan, defensive allocation, free-kick and throw-in options, rehearsal checklist, and adjustment triggers.

📦 **What you receive**

- Two conditional corner routines with delivery zones, movement roles, and second-ball coverage.
- A defensive-corner allocation with first-contact and transition priorities.
- Free-kick and throw-in opportunities sized to the information you supplied.
- Clear `[ADD]` and `[UNVERIFIED]` labels where your fact pack is incomplete.

💡 **What makes it different**

1. It separates attacking and defensive set-piece decisions instead of offering generic match commentary.
2. It ends with practical rehearsal cues and in-match triggers, so the brief can support a coaching conversation.
3. **Honesty:** it reasons only from your pasted input and model knowledge; it does not claim live team news, fixtures, or statistics.

👤 **Who it’s for**

Coaches, analysts, supporters, and football content creators who want a disciplined set-piece plan from the notes they already have.
