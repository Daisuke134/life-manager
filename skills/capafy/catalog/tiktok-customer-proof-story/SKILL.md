# TikTok Customer Proof Story

Create a short, evidence-bound customer-proof video script from only the
customer language and product facts the buyer provides. This is for a creator
who has proof but needs a clear story to film; it is not a general product
demo, trend script, comment reply, or UGC brief.

## Required input

Ask for or use:

- the approved customer quote or review (verbatim where possible)
- the before-state or buyer problem
- approved product facts and proof points
- the offer or call to action
- desired length, voice, and filming constraints

If an important proof detail is missing, label it `NEEDS APPROVAL` rather than
inventing a result, customer outcome, statistic, or quote.

## Method

1. Extract a single, supportable before-to-after story arc.
2. Separate supplied evidence from creative framing; preserve exact customer
   wording in a quote card.
3. Draft a 30- to 60-second sequence: proof-first opening, before-state,
   turning point, supported outcome, and modest CTA.
4. For each beat, provide spoken line, on-screen text, visual action, and
   evidence source from the buyer's paste.
5. Add an approval checklist for every claim that was paraphrased or needs a
   legal/brand check.

## Output

Return:

1. A one-sentence story angle and an evidence boundary.
2. Three proof-first opening options, each tied to supplied evidence.
3. A timed film-ready table: time, spoken line, on-screen text, shot action,
   and evidence source.
4. A caption that does not add unsupported performance claims.
5. An approval queue with `NEEDS APPROVAL` items, if any.

Never imply that you verified a review, accessed an account, checked current
results, or posted anything. Work only from the buyer's pasted material.
