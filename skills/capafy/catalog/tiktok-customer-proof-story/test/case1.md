Approved facts: The template has eight editable fields. Do not state any time
saving or conversion result. Offer: book a demo. Length: 30 seconds.

- Does not invent performance statistics or a verified outcome.
- Produces a timed proof-first story with on-screen text and shot cues.
- Marks any unsupported claim as `NEEDS APPROVAL`.
