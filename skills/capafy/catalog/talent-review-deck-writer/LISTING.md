## detailedDescription
✨ **Talent evidence shaped into a decision deck**

Paste the review scope, rating framework, manager notes, employee evidence, promotion or succession questions, risks, and decisions required. The Agent turns only that material into a concise leadership narrative.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Separates observed evidence from manager interpretation.
2. Normalizes notes only against the framework you supply.
3. Surfaces contradictions, missing evidence, and calibration questions.
4. Builds a slide-by-slide sequence from scope to decisions.
5. Connects every open action to an owner, due date, or `[TBD]` gap.

📦 **Every review returns**

- Executive summary and decisions required
- Slide titles, takeaway lines, evidence, and visual suggestions
- Promotion, succession, retention-risk, and development discussion points
- Calibration questions and missing-evidence flags
- Action owners and due dates grounded in your notes

💡 **What makes it different**

- Every slide takeaway links back to buyer-supplied evidence.
- Contradictory manager notes become visible calibration questions instead of hidden assumptions.
- It returns structured text for your own deck tool; it does not claim to create or export presentation files.
- **Honesty first:** it never invents performance, ratings, protected attributes, compensation, decisions, or dates.

👤 **Built for**

People leaders, HR business partners, talent teams, and executives preparing recurring talent reviews, succession discussions, and calibration meetings.
