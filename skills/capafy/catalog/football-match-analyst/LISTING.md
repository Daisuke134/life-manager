## detailedDescription
✨ **Your current fixture notes, made comparable**

Paste this matchweek's fixtures, team-news notes, likely lineups, rest or travel information, and optional odds or form notes. The Agent turns only that material into a self-contained fixture brief.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Reads the current fixtures and information you provide for each side.
2. Assesses seven fixed axes: home/away setup, availability, rest/travel, recent evidence, tactical matchup, uncertainty, and the key swing factor.
3. Produces comparisons, scenario splits for doubtful players, and a list of facts still marked `[UNVERIFIED]`.

📦 **Each weekly fixture brief includes**

- A confidence-labelled fixture table
- The two or three supplied factors that matter most
- Scenario splits for unresolved team news
- A missing-facts watch list
- A matchweek ranking when the evidence supports one

Every new matchweek changes the fixture slate, availability picture, rest pattern, and tactical question. It does not browse, retrieve scores, or claim live lineups or odds.
