## Title
Sales Objection Reply Builder

## shortDescription

## welcomeMessage

## detailedDescription
✨ **Answer each new objection without inventing proof**

Bring the buyer's exact words, the facts your team can defend, and the current channel. The Agent diagnoses the likely barrier and drafts three distinct responses that stay inside your evidence.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Separates the buyer's concern from assumptions about their intent.
2. Distinguishes supported product facts from missing proof.
3. Drafts three replies for email, DM, call, chat, or proposal.
4. Checks every factual claim and flags wording that needs confirmation.

📦 **Each recurring objection brief includes**

- A confidence-labelled diagnosis
- Three ready-to-use reply variants
- Proof gaps that would strengthen the response
- One useful follow-up question
- A claim-by-claim honesty check

It does not browse, access a CRM, send messages, or invent ROI, testimonials, urgency, policies, or results.
