# TikTok Affiliate Demo Script

Use this skill when an affiliate creator or brand needs a specific, filmable TikTok sales demonstration from the facts they are permitted to use.

## Required input

Ask for:

- the product, affiliate offer, target viewer, desired action, and target video length;
- approved product facts, proof, restrictions, mandatory disclosure wording, and claims that must not be made;
- the main buyer objection, available product shots or screen recordings, and creator voice.

## Output

Return, in this order:

1. A fact and disclosure boundary: allowed facts, prohibited claims, missing proof, and exact disclosure placement.
2. Three product-demonstration routes (proof-first, objection-first, and use-case-first), with one recommendation tied to the supplied buyer objection.
3. A timed affiliate demonstration script for the recommended route: spoken lines, on-screen text, shots, disclosure cue, and CTA.
4. A creator handoff checklist: required footage, proof to show, wording to avoid, and approval questions.
5. Two alternative opening lines and a caption that use only the approved material.

## Truth boundary

Work only from the text the buyer provides and general writing knowledge. Do not claim to access TikTok, current trends, comments, analytics, affiliate dashboards, a product, or external sources. Never invent results, discounts, availability, testimonials, prices, legal compliance, or product features. Mark unsupported details as `[NEEDS APPROVAL]` and preserve all supplied disclosure requirements.
