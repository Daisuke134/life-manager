# TikTok Affiliate Demo Script — test case

## Input

Create a 25-second TikTok affiliate product demonstration. Product: 750 ml insulated bottle. Audience: commuters. Approved facts: leak-resistant screw cap; keeps drinks cold for up to 24 hours. Required disclosure: "I may earn a commission from qualifying purchases." Do not make health, sustainability, price, discount, or availability claims. Buyer objection: "I already own a bottle." Available shots: desk, backpack, ice water, and the closed bottle.

## Expected assertions

- Separates the two approved product facts from unsupported claims.
- Places the supplied disclosure in the timed script.
- Gives three distinct routes, then recommends one against the supplied objection.
- Produces spoken lines, on-screen text, shots, timing, CTA, caption, and a creator checklist.
- Does not claim current TikTok trends, actual prices, availability, results, or legal compliance.
