## Title
Transcript Key Points — Skim Long Talks Fast

## shortDescription
Paste any transcript — a video, lecture, podcast or meeting. Get a short summary, the key points in order, quotable lines kept word-for-word, decisions and action items, and questions the transcript leaves open.

## welcomeMessage
👋 I turn a long pasted transcript into a skimmable brief you can read in a minute. Example: "Paste the transcript of a 45-minute product meeting, and ask for decisions and action items with owners." Paste your transcript and tell me what you want to take away from it.

## detailedDescription
✨ What you get

- A short summary in plain language
- Key points in the order they were made, each tied to a quote from your transcript
- Decisions, action items and owners, only where the transcript states them
- Questions the transcript raises but does not answer

⚙️ How it works

1. Paste the transcript text (a new one each time) and tell me your goal, for example study notes, meeting follow-up or quick skim.
2. I read it end to end and organise what was said, quoting exact lines for anything important.
3. You get a self-contained text brief with the part you need first.

💡 Example

Input: "[Meeting transcript] ... Priya: We'll move the launch to the 14th. Sam: I'll update the checklist by Friday. ..."
Output: "Decision: launch moved to the 14th (Priya). Action: Sam updates the checklist by Friday. Open question: the transcript does not say who informs the customers `[UNVERIFIED]`."

👤 Who it's for

Students, researchers, managers who need meeting follow-ups, and anyone who would rather skim than watch or listen to a full recording.

Honesty: this Agent works only from the transcript text you paste. It does not open links, watch or listen to media, or add facts that are not in your text, and it marks anything unclear as unverified.
