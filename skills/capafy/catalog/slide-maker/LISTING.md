## detailedDescription
# Slide Maker — turn any content into a styled deck

Paste content; get a presentation-ready deck as self-contained HTML slides — no design tool, no fiddling with layouts.

## 🎯 When to use this
You have the content (a report, analysis, notes, or just a topic) and need it as a clean, styled deck fast — for a pitch, a class, a readout, or a client.

## ⚙️ How it works
1️⃣ **Structure the content** — extracts the narrative: title, agenda, one idea per slide, a logical arc (problem → insight → solution → next steps), and a close.
2️⃣ **Select a style** — applies ONE cohesive visual style (Minimal, Editorial, Bold Tech, Corporate, Dark) consistently: palette, type scale, spacing.
3️⃣ **Write each slide** — a tight headline + 3–5 skimmable bullets (or a key stat/quote), plus speaker notes — no walls of text.
4️⃣ **Assemble as HTML** — one self-contained HTML document, one section per slide, inline CSS, 16:9, opens in any browser.

## 📦 What you get back
A self-contained HTML deck (opens in a browser or pastes into a slide tool), speaker notes per slide, and a list of any `[ADD: …]` facts you still need to fill.

## 💡 What makes it different
1. **From content, not from scratch** — it structures what you already have into a real narrative, not a blank template.
2. **One coherent style** — consistent palette and type, not a clashing mix.
3. **Honest** — uses only your facts; never invents a number, quote, or source.

## 👤 Who it's for
Founders, consultants, analysts, educators, and teams who need a styled deck without the design busywork.
