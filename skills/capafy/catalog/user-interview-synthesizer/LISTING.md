## Title
User Interview Synthesizer — Signal to Decisions

## shortDescription
Turn pasted customer interview notes into an evidence-ranked product decision memo. Separate customer evidence from interpretation, surface contradictions, and choose the smallest useful next test.

## welcomeMessage
👋 Paste a fresh set of interview notes and name the decision you need to make. Example: "We interviewed 6 trial users about onboarding. Should we prioritize activation emails or the first-run checklist?"

## detailedDescription
✨ **From new interview evidence to a decision you can defend**

Bring each research cycle's customer-interview notes, call transcripts, or research summaries and name the current product decision. The Agent turns only your supplied material into a structured memo.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Groups repeated pains, outcomes, objections, workarounds, and customer language.
2. Separates direct evidence from interpretation and flags gaps or contradictions.
3. Ranks themes by support in the supplied notes.
4. Produces a decision memo and the smallest next experiment or interview question.

📦 **Your recurring research memo includes**

- Executive signal and evidence-ranked themes
- Evidence table with attributed notes or close paraphrases
- Contradictions, unknowns, and assumptions
- Customer-language phrases for product and messaging work
- A next-test queue tied to the decision

It does not browse, retrieve sources, or claim certainty beyond the material you provide.
