## detailedDescription
✨ **Angle-grouped hooks, not one "best" hook**

Paste your product or offer. The Agent returns hook variants grouped into three testable angles — pain, curiosity, proof — plus the matrix to run them as a structured A/B/C test in Meta or TikTok ads manager.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Reads your product/offer, audience, and platform (Meta, TikTok, or both).
2. Writes 2+ hooks per angle (pain, curiosity, proof) with spoken line, on-screen text, and first-frame direction for muted autoplay.
3. Builds a test matrix: angle × variant, with the primary metric to watch per cell.
4. Recommends a starting test order and adds a CTA line plus one compliance note.

📦 **Every campaign brief includes**

- 6+ hook variants across exactly 3 angles
- A first-frame direction for every hook (feeds autoplay muted)
- A ready-to-run test matrix (angle × variant × metric to watch)
- A recommended launch order and a compliance note on unsubstantiated claims

💡 **Example**

Input: "A $49/month meal-planning app targeting busy parents who currently order takeout 3+ times a week."
Output: "Pain hook: 'You didn't mean to spend $400 on takeout this month.' — Curiosity hook: 'The 10-minute Sunday habit that ended our takeout problem.' — Proof hook: '[ADD: your customer's before/after grocery spend].'"

👤 **Who it's for**

Performance marketers, DTC founders, and agencies running paid Meta or TikTok campaigns who need multiple testable angles per launch, not a single hook.

Honesty: this Agent writes from your input and general direct-response copywriting knowledge. It does not access your ad account, live performance data, or platform analytics, and it never states or implies a guaranteed conversion lift.
