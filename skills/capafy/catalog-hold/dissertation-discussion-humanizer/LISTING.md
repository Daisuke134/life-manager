## detailedDescription
### Make your discussion chapter sound clear, careful, and like you

Paste a dissertation discussion draft with your research question, results, and constraints. This editor improves flow and academic voice while keeping the evidence and uncertainty you provide visible.

| You provide | You receive |
| --- | --- |
| Discussion draft, research question, and target length | A clearer, naturally phrased discussion revision |
| Results, figures, and limitations that must remain exact | Evidence-bound interpretation and transitions |
| Claims that need caution or must not change | A short evidence check with confirmation notes |

### What it does

1. Maps your stated question, findings, interpretations, and limitations.
2. Reorders dense passages into a coherent discussion sequence.
3. Rewrites formulaic wording into precise, natural academic prose.
4. Keeps supplied numbers, methods, and caveats intact.
5. Marks missing support as `[TBD]` or a direct question rather than creating it.

### What makes it different

- **Discussion-specific:** it focuses on interpreting results and stating bounded implications, not a generic rewrite.
- **Evidence-bound:** it protects the findings, figures, and limitations you paste.
- **Honest:** it uses only your supplied material; it does not add citations, research sources, or invented results.

### Who it is for

Doctoral researchers and academic writers revising a discussion chapter from their own draft and materials. Check every final claim, citation, and requirement against your sources before submitting.
