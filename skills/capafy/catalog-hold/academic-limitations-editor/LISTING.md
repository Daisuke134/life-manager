## Title
Academic Limitations Editor

## shortDescription
Turn a pasted limitations section into clear, proportionate academic prose while preserving supplied study boundaries, caveats, and uncertainty.

## welcomeMessage
👋 Paste your limitations section, a short study summary, target reader, word limit, and anything that must remain exact. I’ll clarify only the boundaries you supply and flag gaps for author confirmation.

Example: “Edit this limitations section for a public-health manuscript. Preserve that the sample was from two clinics, the 18-month follow-up, and ‘may limit generalizability’; do not add weaknesses or citations.”

## detailedDescription
✨ **Make study boundaries clear without overstating them**

Paste a limitations section and the study context you want protected. This Agent improves organization and scholarly readability using only your text, so caveats remain accurate rather than becoming invented criticism.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Maps the limitations, their stated source, and their effect on interpretation.
2. Preserves supplied design details, numbers, scope, and cautious wording.
3. Groups overlapping points into a readable sequence.
4. Revises for precise, constructive academic prose.
5. Marks missing support or implied conclusions as `[AUTHOR CHECK]` instead of filling them in.

📦 **Every session includes**

- A concise limitations map
- A boundary table
- A revised limitations section based on your pasted material
- An edit ledger for material wording changes
- Focused author-check questions when information is missing

💡 **What makes it different**

- It focuses on the distinct revision job of limitations writing, not broad manuscript editing.
- It preserves uncertainty and study boundaries instead of making caveats sound stronger or weaker.
- It never manufactures flaws, evidence, or remedies for your study.

👤 **Built for**

Researchers, graduate students, and scholarly writers revising manuscripts, theses, preregistrations, and conference papers from their own study materials.
