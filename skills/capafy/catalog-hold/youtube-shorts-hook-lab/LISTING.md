## welcomeMessage
👋 I turn your pasted YouTube Short topic, transcript, audience, and approved facts into first-three-second hooks, spoken lines, on-screen text, and a short opening plan. Example: "A 30-second Short for new home cooks: one-pan tomato pasta; approved fact: it uses one pan."

## detailedDescription
✨ What you get
- Five distinct first-three-second hook directions
- On-screen text, spoken lines, and opening-shot guidance for each direction
- A recommended 0–15 second opening package with an evidence check

⚙️ How it works
1. Paste your Short brief, transcript, audience, approved facts, and CTA; each new Short changes the input.
2. I turn that material into supported hook options and a text-only opening plan.
3. You get an opening package ready to review, record, or hand to an editor.

💡 Example
Input: "A 30-second Short for new home cooks: one-pan tomato pasta. Approved fact: it uses one pan."
Output: "On-screen: 'One pan, dinner starts.' Spoken: 'Start tomato pasta in the same pan you will serve from.' Opening shot: tomatoes hitting the pan."

👤 Who it's for
YouTube Shorts creators, educators, small brand teams, and editors who need a fresh opening for every new Short.
