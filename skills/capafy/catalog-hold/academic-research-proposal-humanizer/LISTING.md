## Title
Academic Research Proposal Humanizer

## shortDescription
Turn your pasted research proposal into clear, natural academic prose while preserving your real evidence, methods, limits, and required claims.

## welcomeMessage
👋 Paste the proposal prompt, target length, your draft or notes, and every fact that must stay exact. I’ll make the prose clearer and more natural while flagging claims that need your confirmation.

## detailedDescription
### Make your proposal sound like you, with the evidence intact

Paste a research proposal draft, brief, and project notes. This editor improves clarity, structure, and academic voice while keeping the evidence and uncertainty you provide visible.

| You provide | You receive |
| --- | --- |
| Prompt, required headings, and target length | A prompt-aligned proposal revision |
| Research question, methods, and real preliminary evidence | A clearer narrative with natural academic flow |
| Facts, figures, terms, and constraints to preserve | An evidence check and focused confirmation notes |

### What it does

1. Maps the prompt to the material you supplied.
2. Organizes the problem, question, approach, feasibility, and contribution.
3. Rewrites dense or formulaic passages into precise, natural prose.
4. Preserves careful language around preliminary findings and limitations.
5. Marks missing support as `[TBD]` or a question rather than creating it.

### What makes it different

- **Proposal-specific:** it connects research question, approach, feasibility, and contribution rather than performing a generic rewrite.
- **Evidence-bound:** it protects your supplied figures, methods, and constraints.
- **Honest:** it uses only the material you paste; it does not research sources, add citations, or promise funding.

### Who it is for

Researchers, graduate applicants, and teams preparing a proposal from their own notes and draft. Review every final claim, citation, date, and requirement before submitting.
