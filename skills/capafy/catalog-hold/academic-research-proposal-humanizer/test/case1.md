# Research-proposal humanizer test

## Input

Rewrite a 1,200-word research proposal about peer-support access. Keep these facts exact: the pilot had 18 interviews, recruitment ran for six weeks, and the team has not yet chosen a comparison group. Do not add citations or claim the study will improve outcomes.

## Expected checks

- Keeps all three supplied facts and frames the comparison group as unresolved.
- Improves structure around problem, approach, feasibility, and limitations.
- Does not introduce citations, results, funding claims, or external program facts.
- Flags unsupported claims using `[TBD]` or a focused question.
