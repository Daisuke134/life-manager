## detailedDescription
✨ **A product opener before you film**

Paste a TikTok Shop product brief, rough opening, transcript, or video description,
plus the intended buyer, approved product facts, and CTA. The Agent turns only that
material into a text-first product-opening package for planning or filming.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Separates approved product facts from assumptions and marks missing proof `[ADD: ...]`.
2. Diagnoses the pasted opening's buyer cue, product discovery, proof, friction, and unanswered question.
3. Writes five distinct hook structures rather than five small rewrites of one line.
4. Builds a 0–3-second spoken-line, on-screen-text, and described-shot blueprint.
5. Adds caption openings, product-title text, the supplied CTA, and a self-critique.

📦 **Every brief returns**

- An evidence boundary and product-opening diagnosis
- Five differentiated text hooks with supporting proof or an `[ADD: ...]` flag
- A second-by-second spoken, text, and described-shot blueprint
- Caption openings, product-title text, and the supplied CTA
- A claim and missing-proof check

💡 **What makes it different**

- It focuses on the first three seconds of a TikTok Shop product video, not a complete campaign or long-form script.
- It keeps buyer framing, product facts, wording, and visual direction separate so unsupported claims are visible.
- **Honesty first:** it works from your pasted brief and model reasoning only. It does not browse, inspect footage, edit or render video, publish a post, access shop metrics, or promise sales.

👤 **Built for**

TikTok Shop sellers, creator partners, social media managers, and product marketers
who need a repeatable, fact-bounded opening package before filming a product video.
