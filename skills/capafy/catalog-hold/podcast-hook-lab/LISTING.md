## Title
Podcast Hook Lab — 30-Second Cold Open

## shortDescription
Turn a pasted episode brief, transcript excerpt, or guest notes into a cold-open diagnosis, five host-ready hooks, a 30-second read, and description leads. It uses supplied material, marks missing proof [ADD: ...], and does not edit audio or predict downloads.

## welcomeMessage
👋 Paste your episode goal, listener, guest or topic, notes, and approved facts. I’ll build a careful cold open, host read, and description leads.

Example: “Listener: new managers. Topic: delegation. Guest note: she began by delegating recurring weekly reports. Tone: candid. Do not claim a productivity result.”

## detailedDescription
✨ **A cold open built from your real episode material**

Paste a podcast episode brief, transcript excerpt, guest notes, or a rough opening,
along with your listener and approved facts. The Agent produces a text-first opening
package for planning or recording.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Separates approved facts and exact quotes from assumptions and missing proof.
2. Diagnoses the supplied opening's promise, tension, and listener relevance.
3. Writes five distinct cold-open structures for the same episode.
4. Builds a 0–30 second host-read roadmap and an episode transition.
5. Adds title and description leads plus a claim check before recording.

📦 **Every episode brief returns**

- An evidence boundary and opening diagnosis
- Five host-ready cold-open options with their evidence source
- A three-block, 30-second spoken-text roadmap
- Title options, description leads, and your supplied CTA
- A quote, claim, and missing-proof check

💡 **What makes it different**

- It is for a podcast's spoken opening, not a generic social caption or video script.
- It keeps host words, approved facts, and editorial framing separate.
- **Honesty first:** it reasons only from your pasted material and model knowledge. It does not browse, inspect or edit audio, publish episodes, access listener analytics, or promise downloads, audience growth, or sales.

👤 **Built for**

Podcast hosts, producers, branded-content teams, and interview editors planning a
clearer opening before they record.
