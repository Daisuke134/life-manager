## The pattern

1. **Hook types that win here:**
   - **Confession:** "I have a confession."
   - **Contrarian:** "Everyone's telling you X. It's not, but...".
   - **Insider secret:** "what big creators don't want you to know".
   - **Specific promise with time:** "give me 59 seconds".
2. **Tool-sale arc:** pain in the creator's own words -> "The solution? [tool]" -> on-screen before/after demo -> result -> one action. The 600k winner is exactly a tool demo, which is our product type.
3. **Real demo, not claims:** the screen shows the actual tool running on a concrete example ("let me show you real time").
4. **Look:**
   - Bold top text that restates the hook for the first 2-3 s.
   - Word-by-word captions on every line.
   - Vertical 9:16, 30-60 s.
   - Face or phone/screen demo; 5 of 6 winners show a face, and 1 is a screen demo.

## Gap vs our current reels

Our daily reels are faceless text-on-dark motion cards with no pain, no tool demo and no captions of speech. Copy the faceless winner instead:
- a phone/screen demo of Hook Lab turning one line into hooks;
- a confession/contrarian opener;
- voiced narration with word captions;
- bold top text;
- a single CTA.
