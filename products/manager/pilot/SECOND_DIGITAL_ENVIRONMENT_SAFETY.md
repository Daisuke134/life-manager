# Safety boundary for the second digital support environment

**Protocol ID:** `MGR-F007-DIGITAL-SAFETY-20261022-v1`. **Status: DOCUMENTED_BOUNDARY; human evaluation NOT_ACTIVATED.** Prepared 2026-10-22 JST / 2026-10-21 UTC. This addresses F007.E06 and F008.E06 in the [detailed Phase06 map](../docs/roadmap/PHASE06_TO_COMPLETION_RESEARCH_JA.md). It does not claim a second real-world welfare effect or satisfaction of the original indoor-facility/PM2.5 tasks.

The permitted digital activity is a factual, accessible route from the existing owned Manager support surface to authoritative mental-health or physical-function/activity information. It is public navigation, not treatment, diagnosis, screening, personalized persuasion, application submission or clinical monitoring. Source destinations remain the responsible public agencies' services. Their availability and instructions must be verified at release time; this document does not assert that a particular help line is currently staffed.

## 1. Routine publication under standing authority

An existing bounded owner grant can authorize ordinary updates without an NPO agreement, facility owner, clinical partner or per-run human approval. Its exact repository/path, validity, budget, source version, revocation and rollback remain enforced by the existing release system. This protocol expands none of those rights. A public agency's URL grants no permission to alter its systems, impersonate it, obtain its client records or send messages to its users.

Before a release, retain an independent baseline and the original ordinary-navigation route; verify exact official destinations, source/version, accessible labels, keyboard focus, language, safe exit and attribution. Use existing Playwright/axe and release readback where available. A passed automated accessibility check is a bounded technical observation, not full WCAG conformance or a clinical outcome. [W3C WCAG overview](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/) is the primary accessibility reference in the [verified source map](../docs/research/COMPLETION_PRIMARY_SOURCES_20261022.json).

The page must visibly offer a way to return to ordinary navigation and an appropriately labeled official help destination. Avoid hidden redirects, repeated prompts, unsolicited platform posts, scraped contacts and pressure to disclose a condition. No questionnaire, tracking-based mental inference or personalized intervention is enabled by this document. Declining any optional research must leave ordinary public information available.

## 2. Content and exposure stop rules

| Condition | Required disposition |
|---|---|
| Source missing, stale beyond the existing release policy, redirected to an untrusted destination or materially inconsistent | Treat the resource as unknown; do not publish a new unsupported recommendation. Use existing rollback/NO_ACTION behavior and retain a verified alternative when available. |
| Content diagnoses, promises recovery, invents eligibility, gives individualized treatment instructions or substitutes for urgent professional help | Block the implicated study/content change; remove or correct the claim within authorized scope. No efficacy claim from source-link availability. |
| Coercive wording, psychographic targeting, covert enrollment, platform spamming or private-person inference | **NO_ACTION** for the proposed feature; keep the public anonymous support route. |
| Material accessibility regression or misleading support destination | Revert or disable the implicated change through the existing bounded release process; independently verify the correction. |
| Credible reported distress, delayed help or privacy harm from an optional study | Apply [the independent safety protocol](MENTAL_OUTCOME_SAFETY_AND_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md), preserve ordinary support, and pause study-specific exposure while a qualified independent reviewer assesses the concern. |
| Insufficient authority, uncertain safety of a proposed high-risk change or inability to validate the destination | **NO_ACTION** for that change; preserve or restore verified ordinary navigation. A model's confidence is not clearance. |

This is a stop boundary, not a claim that all listed tests or live monitors have been implemented. Actual implementation and public readback require their own evidence. A content correction may be made under existing standing authority; it does not require launching a human study or waiting for an external partner.

## 3. Optional human outcomes remain a separate study

The [public-aggregate collaboration protocol](PUBLIC_AGGREGATE_COLLABORATION_PROTOCOL.md) defines consent, local custody of private information, independent roles and approved public aggregate releases. The [mental-outcome safety protocol](MENTAL_OUTCOME_SAFETY_AND_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) defines contact limits, harms ascertainment, professional assessment and stop/resume decisions. Those are required for the optional human study they describe, not routine anonymous navigation.

Mental wellbeing and physical functioning must use separate, appropriately justified measures. WHO-5 describes positive wellbeing over the preceding two weeks; WHODAS has distinct versions and scoring methods for functioning/disability. Use the exact rights-cleared instrument, language, mode, window and scoring under voluntary consent only after the actual study is reviewed. Do not reproduce restricted question banks or infer an outcome from web behavior. [WHO-5](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/WHO-UCN-MSD-MHE-2024.01) · [WHODAS](https://www.who.int/classifications/international-classification-of-functioning-disability-and-health/who-disability-assessment-schedule)

No mental/physical study sample size, actual cohort or valid comparator is established by the financial-navigation proposal. A future separate study must freeze its own units, denominator, baseline, follow-up, endpoint, harms, power limits and analysis before exposure. A WHO-5 aggregate cannot be added to a WHODAS score, financial amount or click count. The existing [welfare crosswalk](INTERNET_WELFARE_EVIDENCE_CROSSWALK.json) retains human outcomes as **NOT_MEASURED**.

## 4. Evidence classification

Report deployed bytes, accessible paths and independent readback as digital-artifact evidence only. Consent, verified human observations and causal comparisons are distinct. With no participant observations, mental and physical outcomes remain **NOT_MEASURED**, participant count **0**, and harm completeness **UNKNOWN_HARMS**. A publicly reachable resource is not proof that anyone obtained care or improved.

Preserve negative technical results and rollback records; disclose missing or inconclusive human results if a future authorized study runs. This document grants no authority over indoor facilities and supplies no PM2.5 measurement. The original F007/F008 acceptance and canonical task statuses remain untouched.
