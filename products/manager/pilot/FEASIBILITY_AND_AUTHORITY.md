## F001 review questions and decision rule

For each domain-specific adapter confirm all of the following *before* piloting:
1. Identity and nature of owner/operator, official authorization method and ability to revoke; own account only if verified, no third-party inference.
2. Precise allowed method/scope, maximum cumulative fees or risk (budget), expiry, terms, rate limits and no private-person data.
3. Source URL, rights, terms, hash, time, unit, uncertainty, sensor calibration, data classification and permitted citation/aggregation.
4. A user/policy-selected intervention set with **NO_ACTION** first; alternatives, non-benefit outcomes and downstream displacement.
5. An independent, timestamped outcome readback bound to the effect, not its executor's status; explicit failure/unknown paths.
6. A predeclared credible comparator, measurement window, sample/power assumptions, effect/CI/harms accounting and stop/rollback threshold.
7. Sufficient evidence for actual intended beneficiary, not a click, API status, zero-filled missing value or simulated money balance.
