# Mental-outcome protection, stopping and independent review

**Protocol ID:** `MGR-D015-SAFETY-20261022-v1`. **Status: PROPOSED / NOT_ACTIVATED. Participants: 0.** Prepared 2026-10-22 JST / 2026-10-21 UTC. There is no appointed clinical/safety reviewer, ethics determination or live mental-health evaluation. The numerical triggers below are conservative proposed operating rules, not clinically validated diagnostic thresholds.

This protocol applies to optional human-outcome research and any study-specific mental-outcome measurement. It complements the [first-study proposal](FIRST_STUDY.md) and [public-aggregate collaboration protocol](PUBLIC_AGGREGATE_COLLABORATION_PROTOCOL.md). It does not impose a partner or per-run human approval on routine owned-internet releases under existing authority. Existing source, privacy, security and release safeguards continue independently.

## 1. What Manager may and may not infer

Manager may store a legitimately published aggregate's construct, unit, population, window, denominator, uncertainty and provenance. It may flag missing evidence or a public study-pause disposition. It may not derive distress, diagnosis, suicidality, loneliness or consent from searches, clicks, writing style, financial status, facial features or an AI-generated profile. It does not administer therapy, prescribe treatment, make a crisis prediction, prioritize benefit access by a mental score, or determine whether a participant is safe.

WHO-5 measures self-reported positive wellbeing over the preceding two weeks, while ONS loneliness and OECD subjective-wellbeing measures concern distinct constructs. A lower score or a worse aggregate can prompt authorized review; it does not by itself establish a clinical diagnosis or prove that Manager caused harm. A null or improved average cannot rule out an individual adverse experience. [WHO-5 overview](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/WHO-UCN-MSD-MHE-2024.01) · [ONS measurement guidance](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/wellbeing/methodologies/measuringlonelinessguidanceforuseofthenationalindicatorsonsurveys) · [OECD 2025](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-guidelines-on-measuring-subjective-well-being-2025-update_9203632a-en.html)

No instrument items or clinical cutoff are embedded in this protocol. Before any future mental-outcome instrument is used, a qualified reviewer must approve the intended construct, language, mode, rights, recall window, response to concerning reports and suitability for the population. Version 1 of FIRST_STUDY administers no mental-health scale. Rights observations and source limits are in [the D013 map](../docs/research/MENTAL_PUBLIC_AGGREGATE_MAP.md) and [verified registry](../docs/research/COMPLETION_PRIMARY_SOURCES_20261022.json).

## 2. Protective operating envelope

Consent is voluntary, accessible and distinct from service access. Participants can skip an item, decline follow-up, withdraw contact consent or leave without pressure or loss of ordinary support. No private answers, narratives or study identifiers enter Manager. The independent operator keeps the minimum authorized information and provides actual support contacts; this repository is not a monitored emergency channel.

For the first-study proposal, contact is limited to days 7, 28 and 58 plus one neutral reminder for each missed contact. No escalation of reminders after nonresponse, gamification of disclosure, personalized fear/shame messaging or attempt to maximize questionnaire completion at a participant's expense. Ordinary public support links remain available in both arms. The protocol never requires withholding urgent help to preserve a control condition.

The operator must provide its normal locally appropriate urgent-support route in the consent and study surfaces, with real hours and contact ownership. If someone reports an immediate safety concern through that operator, the operator's trained staff follow their established professional emergency process. Manager does not autonomously contact a person, clinician, family member or emergency service, and it does not ask the person to wait for a research review. This document supplies no fictitious hotline, staffing commitment or diagnosis.

## 3. Signals and mandatory disposition

For operational recording, a **serious event** is an event such as death, life-threatening danger, hospitalization or major enduring harm that the responsible professional considers potentially connected to participation. **Moderate study-related distress** means a participant-reported adverse experience linked to the study that causes them to stop the activity, request support or describe a material worsening; it is an operational safety category, not a disorder diagnosis. The independent reviewer may upgrade any event and must not downgrade it to protect completion metrics.

| Signal | Immediate action | Review / resumption rule |
|---|---|---|
| Participant asks to stop, declines contact, or reports that participation is making them worse | Stop that person's study-specific exposure/contact immediately, preserve normal support access and offer the operator's approved support route | No re-contact beyond the scope the participant permitted; re-entry requires freely renewed consent and independent review of the concern |
| One serious event plausibly related to the study, or credible immediate danger reported through the operator | Suspend new study assignment and study-specific interventions; the trained operator follows its normal urgent-support process | Independent safety review begins promptly, with an initial disposition targeted within 24 hours; no restart while relation/safety is unresolved |
| At least 3 moderate study-related distress reports in either arm within any rolling 7-day period | Pause further assignment and study-specific exposure across the study; retain the original verified navigation | Independent review examines reports, arm counts, contact burden, information accuracy and alternative explanations; no automatic restart when the 7-day window expires |
| Two participant complaints describing the same misleading, stigmatizing or coercive element, or one independently verified materially misleading support route | Disable the implicated study element and pause its exposure | Correct through ordinary authorized release controls; independent study reviewer decides whether renewed exposure is justified and whether a new protocol version is necessary |
| Consent, rights, privacy or confidentiality failure; private records sent toward Manager; compromised assignment or records | Stop study collection/ingestion and affected exposure; contain further disclosure without republishing the records | Operator's responsible privacy/security and ethics functions determine response; documented corrective action and independent authorization are necessary for study resumption |
| No safety contact, qualified reviewer unavailable, outcome ascertainment lost, or evidence too incomplete to assess a possible harm | **NO_ACTION** for study-specific exposure; preserve access to verified ordinary resources | Continued uncertainty cannot count as safety clearance; resume only after the missing capability and review are established |

These triggers are deliberately independent of statistical significance. A potential serious harm does not wait for a p-value or a public count of ten. A pause concerns the optional study or implicated content; it does not unnecessarily remove ordinary verified support information. Safety fixes remain permissible under the existing standing release authority and must be documented as study deviations if they alter the contrast.

## 4. Monitoring denominator and missingness

The operator reviews incoming concerns as they arrive and examines a local safety summary at least weekly while study exposure or follow-up continues. Record assigned, contacted, responding, adverse-event, withdrawal and unresolved counts by arm. One person with repeated reports counts once in an incidence numerator and separately in an event-count column; the two are not interchangeable. Report event timing and seriousness only within authorized local records. The public summary follows disclosure suppression and cannot include individual narratives.

The assigned population is the reference denominator for safety coverage; a response-based rate is additionally labeled with its responding denominator. Missing safety information is **UNKNOWN**, not “no event.” At each of the day-7 and day-28 contacts, define ascertainment coverage as responding participants divided by all participants due for that contact, assessed three days after the target date and after at most the permitted single reminder. A withdrawal that prevents contact remains unascertained in this coverage denominator; it never justifies contacting the person against their wishes. If coverage is below 80%, pause new enrollment for review of ascertainment and burden. This threshold is a proposed feasibility rule, not an assurance that 80% is sufficient for safety; individual concerns and serious-event stops apply at every response rate. Do not exclude withdrawals from adverse-event reporting merely because their financial endpoint is missing.

If the response threshold is crossed in a small cohort, the operator can apply it locally without publishing identifiable counts. Manager receives a public state such as `PAUSED_SAFETY_REVIEW_PENDING` or `STOPPED_INSUFFICIENT_ASCERTAINMENT`; it does not request the withheld cases. Private monitoring must be operational before the study begins. Public aggregate data alone cannot supply individual safeguarding or prove absence of rare harms.

## 5. Independent review and decisions

Before optional human research begins, appoint a reviewer with appropriate expertise in the intended population, mental-health concerns and research protection, plus an independent methods reviewer where causal claims are considered. Disclose professional and financial conflicts. A product author, founder, shipping approver or autonomous agent cannot self-certify the study as safe or validate its own success. Review authority includes stopping the study even if software or business targets favor continuation.

For every trigger, the operator provides the reviewer authorized access to relevant local evidence. Manager receives only the disclosure-approved disposition. The review record must identify the triggering concern category, discovery and action times, evidence completeness, potential relatedness, known alternative explanations, immediate protection, correction, protocol impact, participant communication within consent, and decision with rationale. Record unknowns explicitly. A reviewer is not expected to prove causality before protecting a participant.

Permitted decisions are:

- **STOPPED:** discontinue the implicated study component or the whole study; report results and harms with the resulting exposure/ascertainment limitations.
- **PAUSED_PENDING_INFORMATION:** retain **NO_ACTION** until stated evidence or capability is available; no time-based automatic restart.
- **RESUME_WITH_CORRECTION:** independently approve specific corrections, confirm rights/consent and support coverage, freeze any amendment before new exposure, and record why remaining risk is acceptable under the actual review framework.
- **NO_STUDY_RELATED_ACTION_REQUIRED:** an independent, evidenced determination that a reported issue does not require additional study restriction; retain the concern and rationale in the record. This does not erase an event or turn missing information into a negative finding.

The initial 24-hour target for serious events is an operational proposal. If staffing cannot meet it, the research remains paused; no staffing or clinical-response guarantee is claimed here. The applicable institution's incident-reporting obligations and professional procedures must be determined before activation and take precedence over this proposed schedule.

## 6. False conclusions, stopping claims and publication

Immediately stop or qualify a mental-outcome claim when the instrument/version is wrong, use rights or consent are invalid, the population/denominator is unclear, the comparison is unsuitable, missingness makes the inference unsupported, or the source retracts the result. A content correction does not require reenrolling people. A score change or public survey trend cannot be presented as “suffering reduced” without a justified design and appropriately bounded interpretation.

No early efficacy finding overrides a safety stop. No model-generated explanation is independent review. Aggregate averages are accompanied by ascertainment, uncertainty, costs, adverse outcomes, withdrawals and distributional limits; unmeasured harms remain unmeasured. Null, negative and halted studies are reported under the same planned release process as favorable studies, subject to confidentiality controls. Withheld private evidence is described as withheld, not fabricated or treated as zero.

The final disclosure-approved report records the protocol/hash, actual participant count, intervention exposure, safety ascertainment, all stop/restart decisions, deviations and independent reviewer disposition. It distinguishes reported events from independently assessed related events and notes that a small trial cannot establish safety for rare events. Manager's P3/P4/P5 claims must pass their existing evidence gates separately. The current record remains **0 participants / no mental-outcome measurement / no clinical endorsement**.
