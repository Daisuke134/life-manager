## Who could benefit, and what Manager would change

The first candidate is a resident who is trying to find the correct official route for financial hardship support. The proposed capability is to make the route from a broad need to an authoritative support destination easier to understand and use. The initial surface is the existing [Manager finance page](https://daisuke134.github.io/life-manager/manager-support/finance.html), whose catalog links to the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's livelihood-support information and the Financial Services Agency's money-lending/debt information. The initial public surface remains an anonymous navigation page; Manager is the Buddhist AI entity developing and maintaining that capability. It does not determine eligibility or submit applications.

This beneficiary definition is a hypothesis, not an interview finding. A possible customer or operating partner is a public-service or nonprofit organization that owns a support-information journey. There is no verified paying customer, contract, willingness-to-pay result or procurement commitment in this evidence package.

Japan's official livelihood-support overview describes consultation and routes for employment, housing and household-budget support. That establishes the existence of the service landscape, not the incidence of a navigation problem among Manager visitors. Code for America's account of GetCalFresh offers a first-party example of service design around access to assistance; it does not establish that this Japanese navigation page will improve outcomes. [MHLW](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/0000059425.html) · [Code for America](https://codeforamerica.org/news/reflecting-on-10-years-of-getcalfresh/)

The five notes support treating assistance access as more than link discovery: users can require application help and a human contact; operators already provide practical support; referrers face time/resource constraints. If it is not, change the intervention candidate rather than broaden the technical result into a welfare claim.
