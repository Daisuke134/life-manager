## Who could benefit, and what Manager would change

The first candidate is a resident who is trying to find the correct official route for financial hardship support. The proposed product change is to make the route from a broad need to an authoritative support destination easier to understand and use. The initial surface is the existing [Manager finance page](https://daisuke134.github.io/life-manager/manager-support/finance.html), whose catalog links to the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's livelihood-support information and the Financial Services Agency's money-lending/debt information. The product remains a public, anonymous navigation page. It does not determine eligibility or submit applications.

This beneficiary definition is a hypothesis, not an interview finding. A possible customer or operating partner is a public-service or nonprofit organization that owns a support-information journey. There is no verified paying customer, contract, willingness-to-pay result or procurement commitment in this evidence package.

Japan's official livelihood-support overview describes consultation and routes for employment, housing and household-budget support. That establishes the existence of the service landscape, not the incidence of a navigation problem among Manager visitors. Code for America's account of GetCalFresh offers a first-party example of service design around access to assistance; it does not establish that this Japanese navigation page will improve outcomes. [MHLW](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/0000059425.html) · [Code for America](https://codeforamerica.org/news/reflecting-on-10-years-of-getcalfresh/)

The GOV.UK completion-rate guidance requires clear transaction starts and completions, with failed or partial attempts included. Manager must define its own task carefully: arriving at an information page is a navigation endpoint, not a completed government service transaction. WCAG is a useful technical reference for accessibility checks; a limited automated suite is not a full conformance claim. [Completion-rate guidance](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/measuring-success/measuring-completion-rate) · [WCAG overview](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/)

The [measurement map](../research/MENTAL_PUBLIC_AGGREGATE_MAP.md) defines downstream constructs. An engineering improvement, aggregate population statistic, individual scale change and causal net benefit are separate records. A missing human outcome remains missing; it is never entered as zero suffering, zero harm or zero effect.

## Proposed operating envelope

This document grants no new authority. The existing [surface inventory](DIGITAL_ENVIRONMENT_SURFACE_INVENTORY.md), source catalog and release controls remain authoritative for publication. A proposed content change must stay within that already owned scope; eligibility rules, third-party official pages, user accounts, payment systems and personal case records are outside it.

For the provisional evaluation, the **incremental paid-spend ceiling is JPY 0**: no purchase, transfer, paid advertisement or new paid service is authorized by this document. This is a spending constraint, not a claim of zero cost. Record actual compute, CI consumption and human review time when incurred. If the work needs a paid service or a broader operating scope, record the new requirement and its authority before including it in a run.

Use the existing release envelope's update limits, expiry and revocation behavior. This product note does not invent a second, competing release policy. An authentic receipt, a source-controlled permission and an independent public readback must remain distinguishable. A local fixture signature is not live authority.

## What would confirm or change the selection

U002 requires at least five actual, consented interviews with eligible potential beneficiaries or operators, as defined by the existing task. No interview answers, quotations, consent records or partner commitments are supplied here. Before U003 can be completed, the researcher should record, with consent and minimized detail:

1. A recent support-seeking task, the point where it stalled, and which organization owned that step.
2. Whether finding the right official destination was actually a barrier, rather than eligibility, documentation, language, trust or service capacity.
3. What a successful support step would mean and who could independently observe it.
4. The burden and possible harm of measurement, including unsuccessful referrals or disclosure of sensitive financial circumstances.
5. The operator's ability and willingness to run a scoped evaluation, and the real delivery and evaluation costs.

Use an aggregate finding record with source dates and a clear separation between participant observations and the team's interpretation. Do not publish identifiable case histories. Five interviews would support a decision about the candidate; they would not demonstrate a causal effect.

Reconsider this wedge if interviews show that navigation is not a material barrier, if authoritative information cannot be kept current, if an operator cannot observe a meaningful support endpoint, or if the page directs people toward services they cannot access. Pause a release when a resource is unverified, scope expands, or a privacy/accessibility defect creates avoidable harm. If no credible downstream evaluation is feasible, retain the artifact as a navigation prototype and report the outcome as unmeasured.
