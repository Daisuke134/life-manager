# Living Book / Lab Teaching Roadmap — Behavior × AGI → Manager

**Date:** 2026-10-21. Original publication plan based on actual source-by-source literature, not a claim of 8/9/10 books released or a promised revenue cadence.

## Volume I: How to Model Human Behavior

Reader: beginner engineer or applied researcher. Job: understand what a probability distribution, population, behavioral model and LLM persona each represent.

Chapters: (1) A world of choices; (2) Conditional probability and discrete action space; (3) Population sampling and weighting, IPF; (4) Bayesian networks, tabular GAN/VAEs/diffusion; (5) Behavior prediction and preference models; (6) Generative agents with memory and planning; (7) Networks and social influence; (8) Reproducible instruments and EDSL; (9) Exercises, synthetic datasets, limitations. Use slides 3–11, 26–29.

## Volume II: Can We Trust a Simulation?

Job: tell a plausible generated response apart from human-referenced, decision-grade evidence.

Chapters: (1) Choice distributions; (2) TVD/Brier/Wasserstein; (3) Study-disjoint holdout; (4) Human self-retest; (5) Eleven validity tests; (6) Experimental effects and counterfactuals; (7) Company approaches—Simile, Aaru, Radiant; (8) HeartSteps mismatch and research reproducibility; (9) Real 2011/2012 aggregate observations; (10) Fairness and group failures. Use slides 12–33.

## Volume III: From Simulated Behavior to Verifiable Public Benefit

Job: design an autonomous, rights-respecting Manager that does not mistake tool success for human welfare.

Chapters: (1) The distinction from personal assistants; (2) Legitimate authority and standing permissions; (3) NO_ACTION and uncertainty; (4) Independent readback; (5) Counterfactual evaluation of benefit; (6) External harms, spillovers and cost; (7) Open Blessing receipts; (8) Safety and governance; (9) Preregistered research proposal; (10) Honest road to field experiments. Use slides 34–44.

## Research and publication contract

Each volume must be original text, cite primary sources, disclose unsupported or negative findings, avoid copying third-party copyrighted figures or full texts, and include reproducible methods where possible. Daily research-brief signals become candidates, not automatic scientifically certified chapters. Demand validation before premium product launch; no invented sales, subscribers or public beneficiary count. Books/chapters can be sold only using owned or appropriately licensed source material and with actual provider/readback receipts. Do not confuse numbered Manager Phase08/09/10 implementation phases with chapter/volume numbers.
