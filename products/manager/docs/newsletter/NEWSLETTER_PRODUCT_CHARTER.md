# Behavior × AGI Research Brief — Newsletter and Educational Product Charter

Date: 2026-10-21. This document is **not** a proof of subscriber traction, revenue, or live social-platform posting.

## Mission, reader and editorial job

Audience: a researcher, founder, scientist or advanced practitioner tracking human behavioral foundation models, synthetic populations, social simulation, intervention science and AGI safety.

Reader's job: avoid hours of doomscrolling while staying technically current, knowing which findings are experimentally grounded and which are vendor statements, and obtaining trustworthy links to new code and datasets.

Default issue: concise Japanese explanation, first-source English links and research methods, with a polished English paragraph and X post candidate. Track local JST date and article/paper original issue date separately. News 24–72h prioritized; if no truly new signal, say so and unpack a foundational study.

## Reusable research assembly line

1. **Watch:** papers (arXiv, scholarly publication updates), source release/commits, competitor official blogs, primary technical docs, datasets and benchmark reports. Track Societies.ai/Radiant, Simile, Aaru, Expected Parrot, SocSci210, Concordia, AgentTorch, AgentSociety, Generative Agents, DoWhy and other relevant sources.
2. **Provenance:** store canonical URL, authors, version/date and source type (peer-reviewed paper, preprint, company report, code, observable third-party measurement).
3. **Extract:** mechanism, inputs, training, evaluation, real-world behavior comparator, surprising/negative results, limitations, significance for Manager.
4. **Distinguish:** population metrics vs individual fit; association vs treatment effect; simulation vs live action; execution receipt vs independently measured effect.
5. **Deliver:** daily brief, annotated background card, single actionable research experiment, publish-ready original article concept, original editable visual, short X candidate.
6. **Publish:** only after source rights and editorial gates; use the established reviewed Anicca Manager blog bridge with live URL verification. Social cross-posting requires a connected provider and public receipt; never label a draft a published post.
7. **Measure:** source freshness, error corrections, reading completion, source clicks, replies, qualified newsletter signups, paid orders/refunds net of costs. Never invent subscriber numbers.

## Free and potential paid layers

- **Open/free:** daily news digest with sources; annotated field glossary; public evidence cards and open visual research map.
- **Potential paid:** long-form methodological replications, source notebooks, slide packs, benchmark comparison reports, beginner-to-researcher book series, member Q&A and training sessions.
- **First marketing metric:** subscriber signup verified by a real provider. Future paid outcome: actual settled contribution = received revenue − payment fees − refunds − production costs; distinguish from marketing clicks and hypothetical demand.
- **No monetization without confirmed redistribution rights** for third-party material. Original diagrams/code/notes can be reused subject to their own licenses.

## Product learning order

1. Why behavior prediction is hard → population synthesis and measurement
2. Conditional choice models → LLM behavior models and fine-tuning
3. Generative agents / network influence → interactive societies
4. Prediction metrics and holdout design → validity/causal fidelity
5. Micro-randomized and field interventions → spillovers and harms
6. Evidence receipts and transparent welfare → Manager as research proposal
7. Source-specific company deep-dives: Radiant / Simile / Aaru / Expected Parrot, etc.
8. Convert validated chapters into reusable books and a living lab presentation; update the same materials, do not start from scratch at every news event.

## Editorial acceptance

- Each numerical scientific claim is linked to a source, source type and exact metric denominator.
- Company metrics are marked as claimed unless independently reproduced.
- A fictional example is not called a measured study.
- No private participant data is published; avoid identifying or targeting individuals from public sources.
- X/social publication is DISABLED when no connected channel with a successful posting receipt is present.
- No Manager benefit or billion-dollar revenue claim absent verified external records.
