## Question and competing hypotheses (imozuru method)

Question: Can a metadata-only offline gate screen a proposed *already public* group-level release before a Manager operator considers independent source retrieval, without suggesting that a fixed anonymity threshold guarantees disclosure control?

- Hypothesis A (naive, falsified by the examples): if a cell is hidden and all visible counts are 10 or more, the table is automatically safe.
- Hypothesis B (supported for a narrow class of partition tables): published totals and complements can reconstruct a suppressed count, even when the suppressed cell itself is not printed. A local check can catch some of these arithmetic cases.
- Important alternative explanation and limit: many disclosure risks arise from auxiliary or overlapping releases and contextual knowledge; these **cannot be ruled out** by a check of one table. The source publisher and an independent disclosure reviewer must assess actual public combinations.

## Primary-source trace (read on 2026-10-23)

1. **UK Office for National Statistics**, *Policy on protecting confidentiality in tables of birth and death statistics* (primary official policy, specific to birth/death tabulations): https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/methodologytopicsandstatisticalconcepts/disclosurecontrol/policyonprotectingconfidentialityintablesofbirthanddeathstatistics . The policy explicitly treats information combinations and third-party knowledge as possible disclosure mechanisms, recommends an audit trail of released tables, and warns that small numbers are **not necessarily** disclosive without context. **Implementation consequence:** block single-cell reconstruction from an exact total; do not label a raw minimum threshold as proof of anonymity. **Scope limit:** do not claim this ONS policy establishes general legal requirements for a Japanese Manager study.
2. **UK Information Commissioner's Office**, *How do we ensure anonymisation is effective?* (primary official guidance, marked as under review): https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-sharing/anonymisation/how-do-we-ensure-anonymisation-is-effective/ . The guidance discusses singling out, linkage, a motivated-intruder risk review, other accessible information, and contextual judgment. **Implementation consequence:** an aggregate-only cell count test always returns `disclosure_certified: false` and cannot authorise ingestion. **Scope limit:** the ICO says this guidance may change following legislative updates; it is not an exact numeric anonymity rule.
3. **US HHS Office for Human Research Protections**, *Engagement of Institutions in Human Subjects Research (2008)* (primary official US federal guidance, non-binding except for specified regulatory requirements): https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/regulations-and-policy/guidance/guidance-on-engagement-of-institutions/index.html . Institutional participation and review obligations depend on actual research activities, the applicable regulatory jurisdiction, and legal requirements. **Implementation consequence:** declared publisher metadata cannot authenticate partner approval, ethics, or consent. **Scope limit:** do not apply HHS HHS-funded research rules universally to Japan.
4. **Internal original protocol**, `pilot/PUBLIC_AGGREGATE_COLLABORATION_PROTOCOL.md` (2026-10-22), and D013's primary-source map at `docs/research/MENTAL_PUBLIC_AGGREGATE_MAP.md`. These define Route A (already publicly released aggregates) versus Route B (partner-mediated approved releases), the no-person-row boundary, and the proposed `MIN_CELL = 10` screening heuristic. Their publication is **not** partner consent.

## Implementation and reproducible synthetic counterexamples

`manager_core/public_aggregate_preflight.py` adds `preflight_public_aggregate(packet)` as a **pure, deterministic, network-free metadata screen**. It reuses the existing `Source` contract to reject private/unknown source privacy and unapproved rights; it checks exact schemas and non-person partition counts; it screens cell-count size, sensitive zero counts, partition consistency and simple suppressed-complement reconstruction; it flags nonpublic releases, individual-level material, missing overlap review, and collaborator studies needing independent evidence. Unknown/external validation is never filled with invented values. It does **not** fetch, publish or store an aggregate, simulate an intervention or calculate a welfare effect. The function returns only fixed reason codes, not group labels, counts or source text.

Test examples are completely synthetic: 100 = 60 + 40 (passes metadata only), 100 = 93 + 7 (reject small cell), 100 = 40 + [suppressed 60] (reject reconstructable complement), 100 = 96 + [two suppressed summing 4] (reject), and 100 = 50 + two suppressed summing 50 (needs external review). Every outcome has `can_ingest=false`, `can_publish=false`, `can_execute=false`, `disclosure_certified=false`, `beneficiary_effect=NOT_MEASURED`. Even a positive preliminary result **cannot** assert consent or ethically clear Route B.

**Local isolated targeted run (not the hosted full repository suite):** `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_public_aggregate_preflight.py' -v` — 15 passed, 0 failures, 2026-10-23. The isolated test environment used a stub for imported `Source`/URL checks because this container cannot access GitHub; the full upstream implementation must be verified by hosted GitHub Actions after commit. No actual published aggregate bytes or human records were fetched.

## Falsification / next decision

The check can miss disclosure from overlapping tables with different partitions, rare attributes, prior versions, joinability to external releases, differencing over time, or intentional misstatement of publisher/source authority. These must remain independent release-review tasks. D014 remains `todo` until actual legal/ethics/consent requirements and public release conditions are met. No external organization needs to be contacted merely to advance offline design. The broader product stays **End suffering / macro-environment / no routine human-in-the-loop**, while the separate research protections remain binding on human-participant studies. H005 is scientifically unresolved, never a reproduced 2019 RCT.
