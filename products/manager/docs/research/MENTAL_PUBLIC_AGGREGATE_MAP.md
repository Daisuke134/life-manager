# Public indicators and validated constructs for mental, physical and financial outcomes

**D013 evidence artifact — literature and measurement map.** Reviewed 2026-10-22 JST / 2026-10-21 UTC. This document maps public aggregate indicators, instrument documentation and their limits. It contains no private-person profiles, inferred diagnoses, questionnaire responses or participant-level data. It does not start a human study or satisfy D014's ethical and institutional requirements.

The purpose is to decide what an observation means before Manager uses it as evidence. A public aggregate describes a defined population. An instrument score measures a specified construct under a specified administration procedure. A delivered web page demonstrates an artifact state. None of these alone establishes that Manager improved a person's life. The product's [P0–P5 definitions](../MANAGER_PRODUCT_SPEC.md) continue to apply; human outcomes in the current digital service are **NOT_MEASURED**.

## Measurement map

## A public aggregate example, with the boundary made explicit

The official Community Life Survey reports **6.6%** of England adults aged 16+ feeling lonely often or always during October 2024–March 2025; the headline rounds this to 7%. This is a published population estimate from that survey, not a count of Manager beneficiaries. Its population, timing and geography differ from a prospective Japanese support-navigation study. No comparison with Manager visitors or attribution to the prototype is valid. [Official publication, 10 December 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/community-life-survey-202425-annual-publication/community-life-survey-202425-loneliness-and-support-networks)

If this number is stored, its record must carry: `population=England adults 16+`, the exact survey window, `unit=percent`, the response category, the source URL/version, and `relation_to_manager=context_only`. Retain the source's uncertainty and weighting metadata when importing its tables; do not manufacture a standard error from the rounded percentage. The source's descriptive comparisons are not evidence about Manager's causal effect.

The same rule applies to support-network percentages: availability of someone to call does not establish whether a specific person received useful help. Small-area disaggregation, linkage to browsing traces and attempts to infer an individual's loneliness or financial hardship are outside this map.

## Conditions for a future result to support a claim

Before collecting any outcome, a protocol must identify the beneficiary population, sampling/assignment unit, exact outcome and version, observation window, comparator, estimator and uncertainty. It must specify how costs, adverse outcomes, service displacement, nonresponse and dropouts are reported. A repeated score requires the same justified administration procedure; a translated instrument requires evidence relevant to that language and setting. These decisions belong in the existing preregistration/ethics process, not in this literature map.

The minimum resulting record should distinguish:

- **Construct and value:** original unit, direction, version and any transformation, rather than an invented common “blessing score.”
- **Observation provenance:** actual respondent or legitimate aggregate source, dates, coverage, sample size/denominator and evidence link; privacy-preserving publication must not erase the provenance needed by authorized reviewers.
- **Inference:** descriptive change, estimated causal effect and forecast are separate fields; assumptions, intervals and sensitivity must accompany a causal claim.
- **Absence:** missing, withheld, not collected and failed verification remain explicit states. An unmeasured harm is not a zero-harm observation.

An official survey can provide context without being part of Manager's evidence ladder. Manager's current service has real digital-delivery evidence at **P2 artifact only**, while its mental, physical and financial human outcomes remain **NOT_MEASURED**. Reaching P3 requires authentic downstream observations. Reaching P4 requires credible causal net improvement, and P5 requires independent replication as specified by the product gates.
