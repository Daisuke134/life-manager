## H005: fail-closed mismatch

Klasnja et al.'s published analysis (PMC6401341, Results 'Included Decision Points') reports:
- 8,274 decisions, minus 390 travel/technical points, minus 340 after 42-day planned end, minus four system anomalies = **7,540**.
- Of those, 1,479 unavailable; **6,061 available decisions**.

The *current* independent Python prototype incorrectly treats **`study.day.nogap = floor(decision.index.nogap / 5)`** as if the two variables were identical. They are not interchangeable without validation. Author `Main Effects Analysis.R` at commit `c077c86d20b5d9cfa937f7ed1720172d726ba9a0`, lines 18–34, reads `analysis-small.RData` and `csv.RData` and filters `!is.na(study.day.nogap)`, `study.day.nogap <= 41`, legitimate users reaching day 35, not-unavailable-and-sent anomalies and `!is.na(send.active)`. The public 2023 suggestions schema gives `decision.index.nogap` instead, and published sample-column names require correspondence. Do **not** infer that five decisions occur every day for every participant, or silently convert missing sends into 'no intervention'.

The last aggregate-only real-data probe showed:
- 390 missing decision-index values, 278 rows beyond day 41 by the unverified `floor(index/5)` proxy, 3 anomalies and 1 missing action.
- Keep missing `send` rows: **7,602 included / 6,118 available with known assignment**.
- Exclude missing `send` rows: **6,997 included / 6,118 available**.
- Original paper: **7,540 included / 6,061 available**.
- A scan of global cutoffs 190–220 found **no exact pair of matches**. These observations come from [failed but artifact-producing run 39447576063](https://github.com/Daisuke134/life-manager/actions/runs/39447576063), not from an achieved replication. The failure is intentional fail-closed behavior; the pure-function tests passed.

The **data-preparation mismatch** is the supported root-cause *category*; the precise mapping has not been demonstrated. This cannot be solved scientifically by choosing a cutoff solely because it matches the paper's totals. Source `Main Effects Analysis.R` also uses a cluster/small-sample estimator not duplicated exactly by our Python prototype. H005 and later H006–H010 remain TODO until the exact analysis population is reproduced or a clearly labeled alternative study is designed and separately approved as a revised task.

Next H005 investigation: locate accessible documentation or original data-processing script that defines `study.day.nogap` and each participant's qualifying study-day limit, compare the logic against the pinned public CSV using **aggregate-only** diagnostics, check any correction for missed/extended study days, and document remaining irreducible discrepancies. If original variables are unavailable, request technical clarification from the dataset authors as professional correspondence (not permission for everyday research) or report exact replication as not feasible with this release.

## Manager philosophy: autonomous by default, bounded by existing authority

**No human in the routine execution loop** is a product invariant: ongoing public observation, research, forecasting, candidate generation, simulation, selection, safe routine execution, independent verification and bounded policy learning should not require ad-hoc per-action human decisions. This adapts the successful Life Manager architectural *principle*, not its runtime, data or business model.

The controller must have standing machine-checkable grants for any owned/delegated budget, infrastructure or outside effect. **No per-action approval** inside that grant. If a proposed action is out of scope, expired, unlicensed, unconsented, harmful or unmeasurable, the agent abstains or chooses `NO_ACTION` rather than impersonating an owner or acting without authority. Owners and affected parties keep revocation, challenge and remedy channels outside the routine loop. An AI does not create its own legal privileges, and privacy/consent boundaries are not bypassed by calling an action a 'blessing'.

Real-world tests remain separate from observational public data and model-driven simulations. No Manager field change or measured beneficiary gain is reported here.
