# D014: public-aggregate differencing risk

2026-10-23. Official ONS guidance warns that comparing two aggregate releases may reveal small differences even when each table has large cells. This requires independent statistical disclosure review and does not establish actual re-identification. D014 remains todo and no live research is authorized.
