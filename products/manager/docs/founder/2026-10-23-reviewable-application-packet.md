### What have you learned from research?

**Draft answer (English):**

> Public research can support a careful diagnostic without supporting a product-benefit claim. In one fixed study-level holdout of released aggregate effects, 35 studies were held out from training. The public predictions had test-study macro MAE 0.0751, versus 0.0616 for a zero-effect baseline. A train-only linear calibration had a point MAE of 0.0470. The paired interval for the raw-versus-zero error difference was [−0.0290, 0.00135]; for zero-versus-calibrated it was [−0.00099, 0.03505]. Both include zero. This retrospective diagnostic does not reproduce the source paper’s full method, establish model exposure was absent, or measure any intervention or human benefit.

**日本語での説明:**

公開集約効果を研究単位で35件ずつ訓練・評価に分けた後ろ向き診断です。公開予測のテストMAEは0.0751、無効果baselineは0.0616、訓練側だけで較正した点推定は0.0470でした。ただし誤差差のpaired区間は両方とも0を含み、優越性は結論できません。原著の完全再現、新しいmodel推論、人への便益測定ではありません。
