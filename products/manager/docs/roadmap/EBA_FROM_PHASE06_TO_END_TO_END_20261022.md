## The precise final acceptance criterion
No finite experiment can legitimately prove that suffering ended *everywhere*. Practical success is a *continuously auditable research and product system* that can: (a) define a valued outcome in a specific population, (b) predict and compare actions on out-of-sample cases, (c) act with valid limited authority, (d) independently observe actual change, (e) estimate causal benefit and harms against a credible counterfactual, (f) revert unsafe policies, (g) replicate elsewhere, (h) open-source the tools while respecting participant rights, and (i) publish honest uncertainty and failures.

**Do not convert missing evidence to zero suffering; never turn a P0/P2 test or corporate revenue into a P4 human benefit.**
