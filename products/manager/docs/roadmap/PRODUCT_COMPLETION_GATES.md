## 0. Why “End suffering” cannot be closed by one task

End suffering is a **normative directional objective**, not a measurable one-time finish line that we can declare with a finite test. A model alone cannot prove an end to suffering. Model development, science, pilots, beneficiary evidence, legal authority, governance, and real-world verification must be kept separate. The “digital Buddha / digital god” is a metaphor for beneficial intelligence, not an authorization for control over anyone.

## First practical wedge — operator access still missing

**Provisional finance:** public grant / charitable contribution amplification with bounded matching funds and public transaction readback. Causal metric: incremental third-party net funds *minus* Manager's own capital, fees and displacement of other causes. **This is not yet an established increase in the recipients' financial wellbeing.** Conditions: licensed, controllable source of funds, operator approval, public auditing, preregistered comparison. Research precedent (not Manager evidence): Karlan & List donor matching RCT; published financial ledgers; transparent donor platforms.

If these permissions do not exist, switch to an authorized shared physical environment with publicly available indoor PM2.5 measurements and intervention permissions. The first use case is chosen by access and truthfulness, not by the code structure. Both are adapters to the same kernel.

### What is the value created?

- Financial blessing: genuinely increased third-party aid or resources received **plus** observable user benefit, not donated gross amount.
- Physical blessing: less harmful exposure / climate risk / dangerous conditions, measured independently, with health benefit reported only if supported.
- Work/income blessing: additional verified real earnings or opportunities net of effort, not a publicly listed bounty.
- Social blessing: access to assistance / community support / reduced unmet need, measured without profiling individuals.
- Mental blessing: public *aggregate* wellbeing indicators with suitable ethical controls, never inferred from private individual profiles. A benchmark of self-reported mindfulness is not the same as ending suffering.
- Institutional blessing: publicly evidence-based improvement of rules or resources via authorized institutions, measured against alternatives and equity impacts.

## Core technology — immutable contracts

1. **Observe** legitimate public data and cite snapshots, freshness, units, licenses; no scraped personal secrets.
2. **Hypothesize** the causal mechanism and viable competing explanations.
3. **Propose** alternative actions including NO_ACTION, estimate costs and harm.
4. **Predict/simulate** as a hypothesis filter only, with descriptive fit and causal fidelity **different scores**.
5. **Authorize** via a grant of actual permission: signed limited scope, time, budget, rollback.
6. **Execute** idempotently with known/unknown provider reconciliation.
7. **Independently verify** public external effects/receipts, never just the executor's logs.
8. **Evaluate** treatment effects with preregistered comparison and quantified uncertainty; measure side effects.
9. **Learn cautiously** on held-out/real-world evidence, shadow releases and rollback; not self-authorizing.
10. **Replicate** across domains/populations and document where failure occurs.

## Evidence and impact proof hierarchy

P0 = theory/simulation/retrospective study; P1 = valid external execution receipt; P2 = independently observed actual change; P3 = evidence of tangible beneficiary gain; P4 = credible **causal** net human improvement vs predeclared comparator with uncertainty and harms; P5 = safely replicated P4 across materially different populations/domains. Track failure, negative impact and missing results with as much care as positive ones.
