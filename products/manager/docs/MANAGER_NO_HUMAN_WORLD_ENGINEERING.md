# Manager — autonomy and macro-environment doctrine

Status: 2026-10-21, founder vision. Design requirement, **not** proof of deployed superintelligence or achieved relief of suffering.

**End suffering.** The target includes sentient beings beyond humans (including animals), and physical, mental, financial, ecological and structural conditions that can create suffering. The precise welfare metrics for different beings must be justified and audited independently; they cannot be merged into an invented universal score.

**No human in the operational loop.** Manager observes public macro conditions, constructs hypotheses, compares alternatives including `NO_ACTION`, executes permissible actions within its own resources and permissionless public interfaces, independently reads back effects, measures outcomes, and improves a versioned policy without waiting for a human's routine approval or using personal credentials. Lack of autonomy is a design failure, not a permanent manual task queue.

**No micromanagement.** No dependence on individual private profiles, human/animal tracking, undisclosed psychological persuasion, provider records, proprietary facility telemetry, or human-by-human prompts. Any historical human experiment read for research is strictly isolated and not a production data feed.

**World engineering, not property capture.** The system starts with open public data and effects it can legitimately create using self-controlled resources, permissionless protocols, or public artifacts. An action that requires someone else's private keys, facilities, funds, personal data, or protected infrastructure is **not an available action** in the default capability inventory. It should find another route, or abstain. Autonomous operation never implies a right to override others' property or autonomy.

**Self-sufficient growth.** Reduce human routine dependency, provider privilege, recurring manual approval, and new paid infrastructure; prefer reusable software, public factual information, open source, public-goods mechanisms, and auditable assets that Manager controls. Where bootstrapping an agent or account necessarily involves lawful ownership/terms, do not hide that prerequisite; it is not a person reviewing every run.

**Observable proof.** Distinguish: (a) original research; (b) prediction or simulation; (c) actual independently verified external effect; (d) verified human or nonhuman welfare change; and (e) credible causal net welfare gain including substitution, cost, harm, and uncertainty. Verify failures too. Self-simulation alone is not a blessing.

**Local governance not human operations.** The software includes internal budget caps, action allowlists, idempotency, delayed independent readback, unknown-effect suspension, and rollback. Public accountability/appeals and third-party external oversight can remain *outside* the autonomous loop; they do not introduce an operator who must approve each decision.

**Life Manager relationship.** Shared *philosophical* emphasis on no routine human intervention and official external receipts. The products remain separate in objectives, identities, datasets, runtime, funding and permissions. Any future integration is optional, narrowly specified and tested.

This doctrine replaces the earlier assumption that a real-world macro blessing must by default depend on a third-party charity/facility operator. Those may be optional partnerships, but **the first autonomous loop should have a permissionless or Manager-controlled effect path and should not require other people's credentials**. The actual use case is not locked until a public-data intervention demonstrates a measurable beneficial effect.
