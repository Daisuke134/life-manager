# Manager — autonomy and macro-environment doctrine

Status: 2026-10-21, founder vision. Design requirement, **not** proof of deployed superintelligence or achieved relief of suffering.

## Reuse → Connect → Evaluate (updated founder direction, 2026-10-21)

**Internet is a real-world environment.** Websites, open-source infrastructure, accessibility, public-goods services, digital commerce, open protocols and voluntarily used self-help resources are part of the real world. The first Manager loop does **not** require owning a factory, requesting access to another building, obtaining charity administrator permissions or developing a proprietary human simulator.

**Reuse → Connect → Evaluate:** Before inventing a model, marketplace, impact score, evaluation runner, intervention protocol or content therapy, search published primary sources, inspect working OSS and license, pin an upstream version, run the maintained tests, and reuse it as-is if possible. Manager's distinctive work is *connecting* existing source → mechanism → capability → safe execution → externally observed outcome → credible counterfactual. Thin adapters, tests, original source attribution and verifiable reports may be authored when no compatible interface exists; they are not claims of new fundamental research. Prefer an existing [Inspect AI](https://inspect.aisi.org.uk/) harness, [GrowthBook](https://github.com/growthbook/growthbook) web randomized assignment, [DoWhy](https://github.com/py-why/dowhy) causal estimators, validated [WHO](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/WHO-UCN-MSD-MHE-2024.01)/[OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-guidelines-on-measuring-subjective-well-being-2025-update_9203632a-en.html)/[CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/educator-tools/financial-well-being-resources/) measures, and the existing Manager Phase05 kernel. Reuse licenses MUST be validated individually; public visibility is not free permission to redistribute copyrighted questionnaire or train on private records.

**Outcome mission: MENTAL, PHYSICAL, FINANCIAL.** A digital pageview, generated post, token mint, opt-in self-help click, browser fix, LLM persuasion score, claimed net profit or PM2.5 observation is **not** a measured decrease in human suffering. Existing primary endpoints include validated opt-in well-being or functioning measures, verified real net purchasing power and reduced financial hardship, and independently recorded health-relevant outcomes; none is sufficient alone. Keep per-domain scales separate, preregister comparator/attrition/displacement/harms, report null/negative/missing, NEVER fabricate a single universal suffering score. **Welfare impact: NOT_MEASURED** without authentic voluntary outcome evidence; scientific Gate1 remains UNMET.

**No routine human approval, but legitimate standing authority:** On Manager-owned digital channels and lawfully permissionless interfaces, an agent can execute under preconfigured purpose/rights, bounded budget, revocation, rollback and platform terms without a person approving each run. Owner/operator setup, required beneficiary consent and external rights reviews where applicable are *setup/legitimacy* conditions, not per-transaction human-in-the-loop. Consent does not authorize covert targeted persuasion, manipulation, spam or exploitation of vulnerable people. Do not attempt forbidden access to other people's Facebook, X, financial accounts, facilities or participants. Use consented/public distribution through assets Manager is allowed to control, with a transparently disclosed purpose.

**Existing world-changing digital pathways (research candidates, not measured successes):** self-controlled accessible websites measured with [Playwright + axe-core](https://playwright.dev/docs/accessibility-testing), transparent self-help information informed by [WHO 2026 psychological self-help implementation](https://www.who.int/news/item/01-06-2026-who-launches-new-guide-to-help-scale-psychological-self-help) (published trials include trained helpers, so fully autonomous clinical efficacy is NOT established), open-source maintenance via [Renovate](https://github.com/renovatebot/renovate) and safe deterministic CI, public official benefits information (without claiming agency entitlement or filing on behalf of people), and owner-funded digital public goods/standards via [DPG Alliance](https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/standard) or existing [x402](https://x402.org/) rather than a newly minted token. The system should never optimize coercive persuasion or engagement at the expense of wellbeing.

**Scientific loop on owned internet resources:** establish a voluntary eligible population and treatment/non-treatment comparator, preregister actual domain outcome before changing the owner-controlled digital artifact, use existing A/B framework, run no-action holdout and independent security/behavior/outcome readback, minimize personal data, follow NICE/SPIRIT/CONSORT and stop/rollback on harm, missingness or legally invalid experimentation. Independent measurement may require *voluntary* human self-report at a study endpoint; that is not a person approving each agent action.

**Source of reusable methods:** [Cross-field source inventory](research/crossfield-20261021/SOURCES.json), [reuse-only methods comparison](research/crossfield-20261021/REPORT_JA.md), and [Phase06 roadmap](roadmap/PHASE06_TO_COMPLETION_RESEARCH_JA.md). **No new task registry**; the existing 113-task `tasks.json` stays authoritative. F004/F007 cannot be marked done by renaming their originally specified funding/site acceptances; treat third-party finance and indoor-site tracks as optional/blocked until actually evidenced, and develop digital-first alternatives without false task-status changes.

**End suffering.** The target includes sentient beings beyond humans (including animals), and physical, mental, financial, ecological and structural conditions that can create suffering. The precise welfare metrics for different beings must be justified and audited independently; they cannot be merged into an invented universal score.

**No human in the operational loop.** Manager observes public macro conditions, constructs hypotheses, compares alternatives including `NO_ACTION`, executes permissible actions within its own resources and permissionless public interfaces, independently reads back effects, measures outcomes, and improves a versioned policy without waiting for a human's routine approval or using personal credentials. Lack of autonomy is a design failure, not a permanent manual task queue.

**No micromanagement.** No dependence on individual private profiles, human/animal tracking, undisclosed psychological persuasion, provider records, proprietary facility telemetry, or human-by-human prompts. Any historical human experiment read for research is strictly isolated and not a production data feed.

**World engineering, not property capture.** The system starts with open public data and effects it can legitimately create using self-controlled resources, permissionless protocols, or public artifacts. An action that requires someone else's private keys, facilities, funds, personal data, or protected infrastructure is **not an available action** in the default capability inventory. It should find another route, or abstain. Autonomous operation never implies a right to override others' property or autonomy.

**Self-sufficient growth.** Reduce human routine dependency, provider privilege, recurring manual approval, and new paid infrastructure; prefer reusable software, public factual information, open source, public-goods mechanisms, and auditable assets that Manager controls. Where bootstrapping an agent or account necessarily involves lawful ownership/terms, do not hide that prerequisite; it is not a person reviewing every run.

**Observable proof.** Distinguish: (a) original research; (b) prediction or simulation; (c) actual independently verified external effect; (d) verified human or nonhuman welfare change; and (e) credible causal net welfare gain including substitution, cost, harm, and uncertainty. Verify failures too. Self-simulation alone is not a blessing.

**Local governance not human operations.** The software includes internal budget caps, action allowlists, idempotency, delayed independent readback, unknown-effect suspension, and rollback. Public accountability/appeals and third-party external oversight can remain *outside* the autonomous loop; they do not introduce an operator who must approve each decision.

**Life Manager relationship.** Shared *philosophical* emphasis on no routine human intervention and official external receipts. The products remain separate in objectives, identities, datasets, runtime, funding and permissions. Any future integration is optional, narrowly specified and tested.

This doctrine replaces the earlier assumption that a real-world macro blessing must by default depend on a third-party charity/facility operator. Those may be optional partnerships, but **the first autonomous loop should have a permissionless or Manager-controlled effect path and should not require other people's credentials**. The actual use case is not locked until a public-data intervention demonstrates a measurable beneficial effect.
