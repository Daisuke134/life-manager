### How to reuse

- **20-minute lab teaser:** Slides 1–4, 9–10, 12, 16–17, 19–20, 22–25, 30, 34–39, 44. Condense for duration.
- **60-minute research seminar:** All sections, skipping nonessential vendor-case slides when needed.
- **Book/education:** Each block corresponds to original research chapters, not paper copies. The newsletter issue becomes a dated change log, not a replacement for evergreen teaching.
- **Future revisions:** Update existing chapter slide content, sources, dates and CI-reproduced figures instead of creating a duplicate deck for each new company.
